## Core concepts
- **VIX**: Cboe's 30-day constant-maturity, model-free implied volatility of SPX from mid-quotes of OTM SPX puts/calls (two expiries bracketing 30 days; 23-37 DTE since the 2014-10-06 methodology change incorporated SPX Weeklys, listed since 2005). Annualized %; VIX/sqrt(252) is the implied one-day sigma (20 => ~1.26%/day; 80 => ~5%/day). Switched from S&P 100 (now VXO) to SPX in 2003; history back-filled to 1990.
- **SOQ**: VIX futures/options settle on the Special Opening Quotation, a VIX built from opening trade prices (not mid-quotes) of the constituent SPX options on settlement Wednesday; it can sit points away from cash VIX (2018-04-18).
- **Realized vol (RV)**: here always 21-day close-to-close, annualized. **VRP** = implied minus subsequently realized variance; positive on average, mildly negative for weeks in slow bears, deeply negative in crashes.
- **Skew**: OTM put IV above ATM/call IV. **Fixed-strike skew** = IV at a given strike; **normalized skew** = (25-delta put IV - ATM IV)/ATM IV; they can move oppositely in a crash. Cboe **SKEW index** (100 = lognormal) prices the left tail.
- **Term structure**: **contango** (far > near) is the calm norm; **inversion** (VIX > VIX3M, or cash > front future) is the stress signature. Ratio used here: VIX/VIX3M (Cboe VIX3M history from 2009-09-18). **VVIX** = 30-day implied vol of VIX options (history from 2006).
- **Dealer gamma**: net option gamma of market makers. Long gamma (customers net sold options) => dealers buy dips/sell rips (dampening); short gamma (customers bought puts in a selloff or calls in a squeeze) => dealers sell falls / buy rises (amplifying). **Gamma squeeze**: call buying forces dealer stock buying, raising deltas and forcing more.
- **Portfolio insurance**: 1980s synthetic-put replication that sells futures as the market falls; short gamma without having bought the option.
- **Vol-targeting / risk parity / CTAs / vol-control annuities**: exposure inversely proportional to trailing RV; when RV jumps they sell regardless of view, with a lag.
- **VIX ETPs**: notes/funds on VIX futures indices (long VXX/UVXY; inverse XIV/SVXY). Leveraged/inverse products rebalance at the close, creating predictable end-of-day demand proportional to the day's move.
- **Variance swap**: pays notional x (realized variance - strike variance); convex in vol, so short positions lose at an accelerating rate (LTCM 1998, Mar 2020).
- **Autocallable**: retail note whose buyer is implicitly short a down-and-in put; dealer hedging of these books is a structural source of index vol supply.
- **Treasury basis trade**: long cash Treasuries vs short futures, repo-levered 50-100x [uncertain]; unwinds when margin/repo terms change (Mar 2020; feared Apr 2025). **LDI**: UK pensions hedging liabilities with leveraged gilt repo/swaps; rising yields => collateral calls => forced gilt sales (Sep 2022). **Carry trade**: fund risk assets in a low-rate currency (JPY); a rate/FX shock forces simultaneous unwinds across equities, FX and vol (Aug 2024).
- **Circuit breakers**: market-wide halts at SPX -7%/-13% (15 min, before 15:25 ET) and -20% (close), approved by the SEC 2012-05-31 (release 2012-107), effective 2013-04-08 with LULD Phase 1; tripped four times in Mar 2020, never since (as of 2026-10-14). The DJIA-point predecessors (NYSE, Oct 1988) tripped once, 1997-10-27. **LULD**: single-security bands with 5-minute pauses; 1,278 halts on 2015-08-24.
- **0DTE**: same-day-expiry options; SPX gained Tuesday (2022-04-18) and Thursday (2022-05-11) expirations [dates uncertain], giving an expiry every weekday. **MOVE**: ICE BofA index of 1-month implied vol of Treasury options.
- **Implied correlation / dispersion**: Cboe COR1M/COR3M measure the average pairwise correlation implied by index vs single-stock options; dispersion books (short index vol / long single-name vol) are short correlation. **Correlation-to-1**: in crashes correlations spike, index vol approaches single-stock vol, dispersion loses.
- **Regime types**: fast crash = liquidity/deleveraging event (days-weeks; VIX > 50; RV > IV by 20+ points; deep inversion). Slow bear = rates/earnings repricing (months; VIX rarely > 40; RV ~ IV; skew flattens). Hedge-demand spike = IV without RV (VIX 25-40, ratio < 1.15, RV < 20%), gone in days (Dec 2024, Mar 2026; Aug 2024 after day one).

## Mechanisms
### Why vol events are self-reinforcing
- Every major episode shares one skeleton: an actor with a **mechanical rule** (delta hedge, leverage target, margin, rebalance, collateral call, redemption) must transact in the direction of the move, into a market where liquidity providers have already withdrawn. The actor changes (portfolio insurers 1987 -> LTCM counterparties 1998 -> quant funds 2007 -> prime brokers 2008 -> HFT inventory limits 2010 -> Treasury PTFs 2014 -> inverse VIX ETPs 2018 -> vol-targeters, basis traders and dealers 2020 -> clearing margin 2021 -> LDI funds 2022 -> carry and dispersion books 2024); the loop is identical: move -> forced flow -> bigger move -> wider spreads -> larger forced flow.
- **Liquidity is conditional**: market makers scale quoted depth to RV and inventory; when RV jumps, SPX option quotes widen 5-10x or vanish (stub quotes at $0.01 on 2010-05-06; no computable VIX at the open on 2015-08-24; VIX 65.73 on 2024-08-05 from wide opening quotes). Treasury liquidity is conditional too (2014-10-15, Mar 2020).
- **Dealer gamma amplification**: put buying leaves dealers short puts; they short futures and must sell more as spot falls. Documented in single names (Ni, Pearson, Poteshman, White 2021) and index intraday momentum (Baltussen et al. 2021); index-level dealer gamma is inferred, not observed, and its sign is contested.
- **Vol-target de-leveraging**: a 10%-target strategy at RV 10% is 1.0x; at RV 20% it must be 0.5x. Because RV is trailing, selling persists for days after the shock (2018, 2020). In 2022 these strategies were already light, one reason the bear produced no VIX spike.
- **Margin spirals** (Brunnermeier & Pedersen 2009): margins rise with vol, forcing deleveraging, raising vol. Visible in VIX-futures margin hikes (Feb 2018); NSCC's $6.9bn of intraday calls on 2021-01-27 that took required margin to $25.5bn (SEC Staff Report) and the $26bn -> $33.5bn Clearing Fund rise DTCC reported for 01-28 (which triggered Robinhood's buying restrictions); LDI collateral calls (Sep 2022).
- **Term inversion**: front-month vol prices the live shock while back months price mean reversion; a regime marker, not a bottom signal. Deeper/longer inversion = more systematic sellers losing carry at once.
- **Correlation-to-1**: in a liquidity shock everything is sold for cash; implied correlation goes to 0.8-0.9+ and relative-value books (LTCM, 2007 quants, 2024 dispersion) that are short correlation lose together.

### The short-vol supply chain
- **Supply**: autocallables and other yield notes; covered-call and buffer ETFs; pension/insurer overwriting; hedge funds short variance, VIX futures or put spreads for carry; inverse VIX ETPs (most reflexive pre-2018); market makers who end up short when customers net buy. **Demand**: tail-hedge funds, pension overlays, vol-target hedges, retail call buyers, long-VIX ETPs, dealers hedging structured books.
- **Dealers warehouse the mismatch**: against autocallables dealers are long vega and sell index vol/variance in calm markets (suppressing IV); as spot falls through barriers the vega can flip and they must buy vol back, a known source of vol-of-vol in Nikkei/KOSPI/Euro Stoxx and increasingly SPX [flip mechanics uncertain].
- **Dated sizes** [all secondary or uncertain]: leveraged/inverse VIX ETP assets ~$4bn at end-2017 (BIS QR Mar 2018), well under $1bn after Feb 2018; CFTC COT non-commercial net short in VIX futures at a record ~150-170k contracts in Jan 2018; US derivative-income (covered-call) ETFs from a few $bn in 2020 to > $100bn by 2025, JEPI alone ~$40bn; defined-outcome/buffer ETFs ~$50-70bn by 2025; US autocallable/structured-note issuance ~$100bn/yr by 2024; vol-control/risk-parity/CTA equity exposure $300bn to > $1tn (Contested). The direction (growing systematic supply, shrinking reflexive ETP supply) is not in doubt; the levels are.
- **Why it breaks**: carry sellers are paid the VRP for convex, path-dependent losses concentrated in the worst-liquidity days; many sellers are also forced de-leveragers (vol-target), so supply disappears exactly when demand spikes (Feb 2018, Mar 2020).

### Episode catalogue (dates | market facts | mechanism | what broke | response | lesson)
- **1987-10-19 Black Monday**: DJIA -22.6%, SPX -20.5% [secondary]; futures fell more than cash as arbitrage broke; VXO (back-calculated) ~150 [uncertain]. ~$60-90bn [uncertain] of portfolio-insured assets sold futures after a -9% week; specialists/OEX market makers exhausted capital; Fed liquidity pledge 10-20. Response: Brady Commission (Jan 1988), NYSE breakers (Oct 1988). Index put skew appeared and never left (Rubinstein 1994; Bates 2000).
- **1989-10-13 UAL mini-crash**: the $6.75bn UAL buyout financing collapsed mid-afternoon; DJIA -190.58 (-6.9%), SPX ~-6.1% [secondary], mostly in the last hour on index-arbitrage and portfolio-insurance-style selling; breakers did not trip; recovered within days. A deal/credit shock gives one -6% day without a funding channel.
- **1990-08 Kuwait invasion**: 08-02; VIX 21.64 (08-01) -> 36.47 close (08-23, the 1990 max; back-filled data), 24 closes > 30 in 1990; SPX ~-20% (07-16 to 10-11) [secondary] into recession and the S&L crisis. Oil + geopolitics + recession = a VIX-30s regime; > 40 needs a funding channel.
- **1994 bond massacre**: fed funds 3.00% -> 5.50% during 1994 (6.00% on 1995-02-01); 10y ~5.2% -> 8.0% [secondary]; Askin Capital (~$600m mortgage derivatives, Apr) and Orange County (~$1.6bn levered notes/repo, Dec) failed; VIX high 28.3 (04-04), -24.0% to 18.13 next day; SPX drawdown < 10% [secondary]. A hiking cycle breaks leveraged rates/convexity books while VIX stays < 30: the 2022/2026 template is thirty years old.
- **1997-10-27/28**: DJIA -554 (-7.2%) on 10-27; the 1988 breakers tripped for the first and only time, early close; VIX close 31.12, high 48.64 on 10-28 then close 31.22 as the DJIA rebounded +337. Breakers stop the clock, not the move.
- **1998 Russia/LTCM**: default 08-17; LTCM (equity ~$4.7bn, assets > $124bn, ~$1.25tn derivatives notional) lost $4.6bn incl. ~$1.3bn in equity vol (short long-dated index vol sold in the high teens, marked above 30 [levels uncertain]); 14 banks injected $3.625bn on 09-23 under NY Fed supervision; first close > 40 on 08-31 (44.28), close 45.74/high 49.53 on 10-08, 15 closes > 40; Fed cut 09-29, 10-15 (inter-meeting), 11-17. Long-dated IV can rise to levels never realized and the mark kills first (Jorion 2000; Lowenstein 2000).
- **2000-02 dot-com bear**: SPX -49% (2000-03-24 to 2002-10-09) [secondary], Nasdaq -78%; VIX closes peaked at 41.76/43.74 (2001-09-17/20, after the four-day 9/11 closure) and 45.08 (2002-08-05); 46 closes > 30 in 2001, 83 in 2002, 39 in 2003. Slow bears give VIX 25-45, not 80; index puts bleed for two years.
- **2007 quant quake**: 02-27 VIX +64% (11.15 -> 18.31); 08-06/09 factor and stat-arb funds lost 20-30% (Khandani & Lo 2007/2011) while VIX only touched 37.50 intraday (08-16, close 30.83): a forced unwind of a crowded long-short book, invisible in index vol. Index IV is a poor early warning of hidden leverage.
- **2007-08 to 2008-09 build-up**: Bear Stearns' two structured-credit funds failed Jun-Jul 2007; BNP Paribas froze three ABS funds 2007-08-09 (VIX 26.48; Fed discount-rate cut 08-17); Peloton's $2bn ABS fund failed Feb 2008; Bear Stearns was sold to JPMorgan for $2/share with Fed support 2008-03-16 (VIX 31.16 on 03-14, 32.24 on 03-17, the pre-Lehman maximum); Fannie/Freddie conservatorship 09-07 (VIX 22.64 on 09-08). From 2007-08-01 to 2008-09-12 every VIX close sat between 16.12 and 32.24: a twelve-month plateau with rising vol-of-vol, which is how a solvency crisis incubates.
- **2008 GFC**: Lehman 09-15 (VIX 31.70); SEC short-sale ban on ~800 financials 09-19 to 10-08 impaired option market-maker hedging; TARP vote fails 09-29, SPX -8.8%, VIX 46.72 (first close > 40); October SPX +11.6% (10-13), -9.0% (10-15), +10.8% (10-28); VIX all-time intraday high 89.53 (10-24), closes 80.06 (10-27) and then-record 80.86 (11-20); 20 closes > 60 from 10-24 to year-end; SPX -56.8% (2007-10-09 to 2009-03-09) [secondary]. Dealer balance sheets (prime brokerage, repo, CDS) shrank as hedging demand exploded; implied correlation ~0.8-0.9 [uncertain]; single-name variance swaps on collapsing financials produced convex losses; the VW squeeze (10-28, Porsche's option-built stake) briefly made VW the largest company by market cap. Response: TARP, Fed facilities, Dodd-Frank, OTC clearing, Basel III. VIX can stay > 40 for months; vol-point put skew shrinks while fixed-strike puts still pay; counterparty risk is a vol input.
- **2010-05-06 Flash Crash**: DJIA -998.5 intraday (~-9%) in 36 minutes from 14:32 ET; E-mini and SPX option liquidity vanished; over 20,000 trades in more than 300 securities executed 60%+ away from their 14:40 prices between 14:40 and 15:00 and were all broken (SEC/CFTC); penny prints came from market-maker stub quotes (secondary sources count eight S&P 500 names at $0.01); VIX high 40.71/close 32.80 (+31.7%), then 45.79 on 05-20 (euro crisis). SEC/CFTC (2010-09-30): a $4.1bn, 75,000-contract E-mini sell algorithm keyed to volume met HFT inventory recycling then withdrawal; Sarao's spoofing (charged 2015) is widely viewed as minor. Response: single-stock breakers (2010), LULD and revised market-wide breakers (2013-04-08), erroneous-trade rules. Option quotes vanish before futures; index "value" can be undefined for minutes.
- **2011-08 US downgrade**: S&P cut the US to AA+ after the close 08-05; 08-08 SPX -6.66%, VIX +50.0% to 48.00; next three sessions ~+4.7%, -4.4%, +4.6% (Fed "mid-2013" guidance 08-09); 75 closes > 30 in 2011; SPX -19.4% (04-29 to 10-03 closes) [secondary]. Whipsaw regimes reward gamma scalping; RV > IV for weeks.
- **2012-08-01 Knight Capital**: a faulty deployment sent millions of erroneous orders in 45 minutes and lost ~$440m; recapitalized within days, merged into Getco (2013); VIX 18.96, unmoved. Response: SEC Regulation SCI (2014-11-19). Operational/algorithmic failure is its own "what broke" category, invisible in index vol and lethal to one firm.
- **2013 taper tantrum**: Bernanke's 05-22 testimony and the 06-19 FOMC; 10y 1.66% (05-01) -> 2.98% (09-05) -> 3.04% (12-31) (FRED); MOVE ~117 [uncertain]; EM currencies and MBS stressed; VIX max close 20.49 (06-20); SPX ~-5.8% (05-21 to 06-24) [secondary]. The earliest clean post-2008 case of rates vol without equity vol.
- **2014-10-15 Treasury flash rally**: with no news the 10y fell 16bp between 09:33 and 09:45 ET inside a 37bp intraday range (FRED close 2.15% vs 2.21%); VIX high 31.06, close 26.25. The Joint Staff Report (July 2015) blamed a withdrawal of principal-trading-firm liquidity and self-trading: the rates analogue of 2010 and the precedent for 2020 and 2025.
- **2015-08-24**: E-mini limit-down 5% pre-open, paused 09:25-09:30; SPY opened -5.2%, hit -7.8% by 09:35; NYSE had opened only 38% of its S&P 500 names by 09:35; 1,278 LULD halts (1,058 in 327 ETPs, 220 in 144 non-ETPs); 40% (20 of 50) of the largest-capitalization ETPs fell 10%+ (36.5% of the ~1,300 smaller ETPs did), while LULD pauses hit 20% of the 50 largest ETPs and 20% of the other 1,491; IVV traded at a deep discount to SPY/E-mini/NAV until 09:43 (SEC DERA 2015). VIX could not be computed at the open (OPEN field = prior close), printed 53.29 intraday and closed 40.74; DJIA ~-1,089 intraday [uncertain], -588 close. Response: LULD band changes, NYSE eliminated stop orders (2016), reopening collars. ETF arbitrage needs an open, quotable underlying.
- **2016-01/02 China/oil/HY**: yuan fears, China's own breakers (01-04/07, scrapped after four days), WTI < $30, energy HY spreads; SPX -14.2% (2015-05-21 2130.82 to 2016-02-11 1829.08) [secondary]; VIX intraday 32.09 (01-20), max close 28.14 (02-11), zero closes > 30: the modal "VIX 25-30 correction" regime.
- **2016-06-24 Brexit**: polls implied Remain so VIX fell 18.5% on 06-23 (17.25) then rose 49.3% to 25.76 as SPX fell ~3.6% (~1.8% on 06-27) [SPX secondary]; sterling -8% to a 31-year low; new SPX highs within weeks. Binary-event vol crushes before the event; a surprise without a funding channel recovers fast ("buy-the-dip conditioning begins here" is folklore).
- **2016-11-08/09 US election and 2017-04-24 French election**: on election night ES futures hit the -5% overnight limit [secondary], SPX closed +1.1% on 11-09 and VIX fell 18.74 -> 14.38 (-23.3%); after the French first round VIX fell 14.63 -> 10.84 (-25.9%). Once the feared outcome is removed, event vol collapses in one session regardless of direction.
- **2018-02-05 Volmageddon**: SPX -2.1% (02-02) then -4.1% (02-05, 3.8 sigma per BIS); VIX 17.31 -> 37.32, +115.6%, the largest one-day % rise in Cboe history (secondary sources quote +104% from another base); 02-06 intraday 50.30; SPX -10.2% peak-to-trough (01-26 to 02-08). Pre-condition: ~$4bn of leveraged/inverse VIX ETP assets at end-2017 (BIS) plus a record non-commercial net short in VIX futures [size uncertain]. 115,862 VIX futures traded in the single minute at 16:08 (about a quarter of the market, BIS) as inverse and 2x ETPs (XIV, SVXY, TVIX, UVXY) bought on the order of 200-300k contracts into the 16:15 settlement [estimate uncertain] and were front-run. XIV fell 84% on closing prices, ~96% on indicative value; Credit Suisse announced an acceleration event 02-06 and redeemed the note later that month [dates uncertain]; SVXY lost ~80-90% and ProShares cut it to -0.5x (UVXY to 1.5x) in late February [uncertain]. LJM Preservation & Growth (short SPX puts) lost ~80% in two days (> $1bn across LJM funds per the SEC) and liquidated; SEC and CFTC charged LJM and its managers 2021-05-27 (release 2021-89). The SEC fined S&P DJI $9m (2021-05-17, release 2021-84) for an undisclosed "Auto Hold" that froze the VIX short-term futures index at stale values in intervals between 16:00 and 17:08 on 02-05, overstating XIV's indicative value as it breached the acceleration threshold. Published rebalance rules are a free option for everyone else; VIX futures, not VIX, drive ETP P&L; size risk in VIX points (+20) not % (+116).
- **2018-04-18 VIX settlement**: the monthly SOQ printed several points above cash VIX (cash range 14.95-16.90 that day; settlement reported near 20 [uncertain]) on heavy opening volume in deep-OTM SPX puts, reviving Griffin & Shams (2018), who document abnormal settlement-morning volume in OTM SPX options that moves the SOQ (Cboe disputes manipulation).
- **2018-Q4**: SPX -19.8% (09-20 to 12-24 closes) with VIX max close 36.07 (12-24; 10-10 +43.9%); RV ~ IV (30.3% vs 30.41 on 12-26); 12-26 SPX +4.96%. Fed hikes plus QT ("autopilot" 12-19), thin holiday tape, Mnuchin's bank calls (12-23). The 2019-01-04 pivot is the clearest post-2008 instance of a "Fed put" the literature dates to the mid-1990s (Cieslak & Vissing-Jorgensen 2021; Miller, Weller & Zhang 2002).
- **2019**: 01-03 Apple guidance cut and yen flash crash (VIX 25.45, the 2019 max close); trade-war spikes of 5-8 points in May (20.55 on 05-13) and August (24.59 on 08-05 as CNY broke 7); 09-17 repo spike (SOFR 5.25%, GC prints near 10% [secondary]; Fed overnight repos resumed, bill purchases from 10-11) with VIX 14.44; three Fed cuts Jul-Oct with VIX < 26. Repo stress is a different channel from equity deleveraging: it needs a Fed response, not equity vol.
- **2020-02/03 COVID**: SPX -33.9% (02-19 3386.15 to 03-23 2237.40), fastest 30% drop ever; daily -7.6% (03-09), -9.5% (03-12), +9.3% (03-13), -12.0% (03-16), +9.4% (03-24); breakers 03-09, 03-12, 03-16, 03-18; first close > 40 on 02-28 (40.11; VIX fell to 31.99 by 03-04 before quadrupling); record close 82.69 (03-16), intraday 85.47 (03-18); 35 closes > 40; RV > 80% on 19 straight sessions (03-17 to 04-13), peak 97.6% (04-06). Vol-target/risk-parity/CTA de-leveraging; dealers short gamma from put buying; basis-trade unwinds and dealer balance-sheet limits broke the Treasury market (10y 0.54% on 03-09 to 1.18% on 03-18 while stocks fell; Schrimpf-Shin-Sushko 2020; Duffie 2020; Vissing-Jorgensen 2021); Fed -50bp (03-03), -100bp plus $700bn QE (03-15), unlimited QE and corporate credit facilities (03-23). Blowups: Malachite, Parplus, Ronin, Allianz Structured Alpha (below). ETP aftermath: cash-front future gap of 10-20 points [uncertain]; Credit Suisse delisted TVIX (announced June 2020) [uncertain]; SVXY (-0.5x) and UVXY (1.5x) survived at reduced leverage. VIX 80 = 5% daily sigma; term structure inverted 30+ points; normalized skew flattened while fixed-strike puts paid enormously; the only reliable hedge was owned before 02-19.
- **2020-21 retail boom**: OCC volume ~7.5bn (2020), ~9.9bn (2021), ~10.3bn (2022), ~11.1bn (2023), ~12.2bn (2024) contracts [approximate, uncertain; 2025 higher]. SoftBank "Nasdaq whale" (reported 2020-09-04): ~$4bn of megacap call premium, tens of billions delta notional [uncertain]; Nasdaq -5% on 09-03, VIX +26.5% to 33.60. GME: short interest 122.97% of float in January 2021 (SEC; S3 Partners estimated ~140% on 01-22); high $483 on 2021-01-28; VIX +61.6% to 37.21 on 01-27 as funds de-grossed; NSCC intraday calls $6.9bn on 01-27 (required margin to $25.5bn) and the DTCC-reported $26bn -> $33.5bn Clearing Fund rise 01-28, when Robinhood restricted buying; Melvin -53% in January, $2.75bn from Citadel/Point72, closed 2022-05-18. SEC Staff Report (Oct 2021): staff "did not find evidence of a gamma squeeze in GME during January 2021" (customer option flow was mostly put buying; market makers were net buyers, not writers, of calls); short covering was a small share of volume and "it was the positive sentiment, not the buying-to-cover," that sustained the rally. Clearing margin, not regulators, stops squeezes; VIX reacts to de-grossing even when the index is flat.
- **2021-03-26 Archegos**: > $36bn gross via total-return swaps across at least seven prime brokers (CS, Nomura, GS, MS, UBS, MUFG, Deutsche; Mizuho and Wells Fargo also reported), each unaware of the others; losses CS ~$5.5bn, Nomura ~$2.85bn, MS $911m, UBS $774m, MUFG ~$300m; Goldman/MS/Deutsche sold blocks first; Hwang 18 years (Nov 2024); CS fined $387m (Jul 2023). First to liquidate loses least; margin must price concentration and liquidity, not just VaR.
- **2021-11-26 Omicron**: Black Friday half-session, SPX -2.3%, VIX +54.0% to 28.62; VIX < 20 within 8 sessions (12-08, 19.90). Thin holiday sessions amplify % moves; an unlevered information shock reverts in days.
- **2022 slow bear**: SPX -25.4% (01-03 4796.56 to 10-12 3577.03); Nasdaq -33%; Fed +425bp plus QT. - **2022-09/10 UK LDI**: mini-budget 09-23; sterling $1.0327 (09-26, record low); 30y gilt yields rose > 100bp in four days; LDI collateral calls forced long-gilt sales (BIS: ~7-10% price discounts); BoE announced temporary long-gilt purchases 09-28 (up to GBP 5bn/day, doubled to GBP 10bn/day on 10-10 for the final week; 13 auctions, GBP 19.3bn bought), TECRF 10-10, index-linked added 10-11, ended 10-14; Kwarteng sacked 10-14, Truss resigned 10-20; MOVE ~150-160 [uncertain]. Convex liabilities + leverage + collateral calls reproduce portfolio insurance in bonds while VIX sits ~30 (Pinter 2023).
- **2023-03 SVB**: $42bn run 03-09, seizure 03-10, Signature 03-12, BTFP 03-12; 2y CMT 4.60% (03-10) -> 4.03% (03-13), 5.05% (03-08) -> 3.81% (03-17), largest multi-day collapse since 1987 (FRED); MOVE ~200 on 03-15 [uncertain]; VIX only 26.52 close/30.81 high (03-13); Credit Suisse to UBS 03-19 with CHF 16bn AT1 zeroed; First Republic failed 05-01. Front-end options carried the shock; index vol stays cheap in a bank scare if the policy response is fast.

## Formulas and quantitative rules
```
VIX (Cboe, per expiry):
  sigma^2 = (2/T) * SUM_i [ dK_i / K_i^2 * exp(R*T) * Q(K_i) ] - (1/T) * (F/K0 - 1)^2
  K_i strike of i-th OTM option; dK_i strike spacing; Q(K_i) MID-QUOTE (bid+ask)/2; F forward via put-call parity;
  K0 first strike below F; R risk-free; T years. VIX = 100*sqrt(30-day interpolation across the two expiries).
  No trade is required: opening quotes 5-10x normal width => inflated VIX (2024-08-05).
  SOQ (settlement) uses opening TRADE prices of the same strips: a different number from cash VIX.

Implied one-day move: sigma_1d = VIX/sqrt(252). VIX 15 -> 0.94%; 30 -> 1.89%; 50 -> 3.15%; 82.69 -> 5.21%.
Realized vol used in this file: RV_21 = stdev(21 daily log returns) * sqrt(252) * 100.

Short variance swap P&L: P&L = -N_var*(sigma_R^2 - K^2); vega notional N_vega = 2*K*N_var.
  K = 20, N_vega = $1m => N_var = $25,000/pt^2. Realized 40 -> loss $30m (30x vega notional); realized 80 -> $150m.

Leveraged/inverse ETP close rebalance (notional): D = A*(L^2 - L)*r,  A = AUM, L = leverage, r = index day return.
  L = -1 and L = +2 both give D = 2*A*r (both BUY after an up day).
  Illustrative 2018-02-05: A ~ $4bn (BIS, end-2017), r ~ +0.95 => D ~ $7.6bn ~ 7.6e9/(1000*30) ~ 250,000 VIX futures [estimate].

Vol-target exposure: w_t = min(w_max, sigma_target/sigma_hat_t). Target 10%: RV 10% -> 1.0x; 20% -> 0.5x; 40% -> 0.25x.
  Dollar selling = AUM*(w_{t-1} - w_t), lagged because sigma_hat is trailing.

Dealer hedge flow: dHedge_shares = -Gamma_net_shares*dS; Gamma$ per 1% = Gamma_net_shares*S*0.01*S.
  Gamma_net < 0 => sell on down moves, buy on up moves (amplifies).

Synthetic put (portfolio insurance) hedge ratio: h = -N(-d1); as S falls N(-d1) rises => more selling.

Margin spiral: max position = Capital/m(sigma), m increasing in sigma; m proportional to sigma => sigma doubling halves position.

Hedge repricing after a -5% day (Black-Scholes, 1 month, r = 0, illustrative):
  5%-OTM put at IV 15: ~0.25% of spot. Same strike (now ATM) at IV 45: ~5% of spot (~20x).
  Fresh 25-delta put: ~0.6% -> ~2.1% (~3x, scales with IV*sqrt(T)). Spreads widen 5-10x on top.

Term-structure stress ratio r = VIX/VIX3M: < 0.9 calm; 0.9-1.0 caution; > 1.0 inverted; > 1.2 crash regime.
  Base rates since 2010: r > 1.0 on 7.6% of sessions; r > 1.2 on 0.5%.

Spike decay (Cboe closes; trading days from PEAK close to first close < 0.5*peak | < 25 | < 20):
  2025-04-08 (52.33): 12 | 12 | 23      2015-08-24 (40.74): 19 | 10 | 29      2020-03-16 (82.69): 19 | 57 | 231
  2008-11-20 (80.86): 27 | 163 | 273    1998-10-08 (45.74): 31 | 31 | 183     2011-08-08 (48.00): 94 | 58 | 113
  2002-08-05 (45.08): 176 | 77 | 192    2010-05-20 (45.79): 45 | 20 | 99
Anchored on the entry a trader could know instead of the peak:
  2008: first close > 40 (09-29) -> first close < 40 after the peak (2009-01-02): 66 sessions; from the first
        80-handle close (10-27, 80.06) to < 40.03 (12-31): 45 sessions, with 20 closes > 60 along the way.
  2020: first close > 40 (02-28, 40.11) was followed by 31.99 on 03-04; from the first close above the eventual
        half-level (03-06, 41.94) to < 41.35 (04-13): 25 sessions.
```

## Practitioner heuristics
- **Size VIX risk in points and SPX %**, never VIX %: +20 points (Feb 2018), +22 over three days (Apr 2025); the +74% of Dec 2024 was +11.75 points.
- **Spike is the trade, decay is the carry, but anchor the decay on the entry you could have known**: from the peak close, VIX halved in 12 (2025), 19 (2015, 2020), 27 (2008 from the 11-20 peak) and 31 (1998) sessions in liquidity shocks but 90-180 in grinding bears (2002, 2011); from the first close over 40 the wait was 66 sessions in 2008 (with 20 closes > 60 in between) and 25 in 2020 from the first close above the eventual half-level. Breaks when the shock is solvency (Sep-Oct 2008), not liquidity.
- **Inversion is a regime switch, not a bottom**: with VIX/VIX3M > 1 stay long short-dated gamma and do not sell the front until the ratio has fallen for 3+ sessions. Base rates: > 1 on 7.6% of sessions since 2010, > 1.2 on 0.5%; every > 1.25 reading sat inside a closes-> 40 regime, while the 2024-08, 2024-12 and 2026-03 inversions (max 1.14, 1.14, 1.07; 4, 1 and 3 sessions) reversed within days. Exception: scheduled-event inversions un-invert on the event regardless of outcome.
- **Ignore cash VIX for the first 15-30 minutes** and whenever cash minus front future exceeds ~10 points; use the front future, VIX1D and SPX mid-quote widths (2015-08-24, 2024-08-05, 2025-04-07 open 60.13).
- **Evaluate hedges at the strike, not the delta**: when ATM IV triples, normalized skew flattens while every fixed-strike put gains.
- **Own the hedge before the catalyst, and quote it in fixed-strike terms**: after the first -5% day the hedge reprices on three axes, vol (~3x), moneyness (a 5%-OTM strike is now ATM) and spread (5-10x wider); a 1-month 5%-OTM put goes from ~0.25% of spot at IV 15 to ~5% at IV 45 (~20x), a fresh 25-delta put only ~3x.
- **Rolled puts are negative carry and need a monetization rule**: PPUT lagged the SPX price index by 2-9 points in most years and did not help in 2022 (-19.8% vs -19.4%); it beat SPX in 2008, 2018 and 2020 because those spikes lasted weeks, while the 2018, 2024 and 2025 inversion windows were 1-16 sessions and a program without an explicit exit gave the gain back (Israelov 2019, "Pathetic Protection"). State a tail program as annual carry (~1-2% of notional a year for a 3-6 month 10%-OTM put rolled quarterly at VIX < 15 [illustrative, uncertain]) plus a written exit trigger (ratio falling for 3 sessions, or a target multiple).
- **Monetize gamma into the headline**: the largest crushes (2025-04-09, 2024-08-06, 2010-05-10, 2008-10-20, 2017-04-24, 2016-11-09) followed policy, electoral or technical relief; exit windows are hours.
- **Diagnose the bear in week one by observable mechanics, never by the size of the first down day**: (1) Funding/solvency crash (2008, 2020): market-wide breakers trip, repo/basis/credit break, VIX 60-80+, RV above IV by 20+ points, ratio > 1.25 for weeks; sell the first crush, buy the second. (2) Policy/positioning shock (2018-02, 2018-12, 2024-08, 2025-04): VIX 35-60, ratio 1.1-1.33, no breaker, no repo stress, a reversible decision or a known rebalance behind it; the first crush is the exit for longs and the entry for short gamma. (3) Rates/earnings repricing (1994, 2013, 2022, 2023-10): VIX 25-36 ceiling, RV ~ IV, ratio <= 1.05; sell vol rallies, own put spreads, not puts. (4) Hedge-demand spike (2026-03, 2024-12; 2024-08 after day one): VIX 25-40 with RV < 20 and ratio < 1.15; short front-month gamma against long back-month vega is the trade, which is roughly flat in regime 3 and fatal in regime 1.
- **Correlation jumps before vol in de-grossing** (Aug 2007, Jan 2021, Aug 2024): VIX can rise 30-60% with the index flat; watch Cboe implied correlation (COR1M/COR3M) and factor returns; record-low implied correlation is the crowded-dispersion warning.
- **Known mechanical flows get front-run**: inverse ETPs, month-end vol-target rebalances, index reconstitutions, VIX settlement mornings; the close-move exceeds what AUM alone implies.
- **Binary events are rarely binary**: Brexit, Dec-2024 FOMC and Apr-2025 realized 2-3x the implied move with one-sided positioning, while Nov-2016 and Apr-2017 crushed vol in one session. Compare the implied event move (straddle) with the distribution of past absolute event-day moves for that event type (e.g. the last 12 FOMCs) and sell only when implied sits above the 75th percentile and skew is two-sided [heuristic, untested].
- **RV minus VIX sets the regime, by magnitude not sign**: the ex-post VRP goes negative in slow bears too, by a few points for weeks (2022: forward RV > VIX on 92/251 sessions; trailing RV 34.3% vs VIX 31 on 05-18), and in fast crashes by 20-40 points for 3-5 weeks (2020: RV > 80% for 19 straight sessions, peak 97.6%). Track 21d RV minus VIX daily: -3 to -8 is a bear-market fact; -20 or worse is a liquidity crash; +10 or more with VIX > 25 is a hedge-demand spike (Mar 2026: VIX 31 vs RV 15). Short-vol carry in a slow bear is positive on average but not every month.
- **Rates-vol regimes do not require equity-vol regimes** (1994; 2013; 2014-10-15; SVB: MOVE ~200 vs VIX 26; LDI: gilt crisis vs VIX ~30; Oct 2023 and Sep-Oct 2026: 10y at 5% and a hike vs VIX < 22); hedge cross-asset separately.

## Common misconceptions and pitfalls
- "VIX is fear": it is the price of 30-day SPX variance, set by hedging demand and dealer inventory; it spikes on de-grossing without fear (2021-01-27) and stays flat in real crises (SVB, Sep 2019 repo).
- "VIX doubled so my options doubled": option price ~ IV x sqrt(T); 30-day VIX and a 0DTE's IV are different numbers; VIX ETPs track futures, which rose far less than cash on 2024-08-05.
- "XIV died because VIX spiked": it was accelerated because the front-two VIX futures roughly doubled; the futures are the product.
- "Skew always steepens in a crash": in vol points yes, normalized often no (Oct 2008, Mar 2020); specify the measure.
- "0DTE causes crashes": no crash since 2022 is attributable to 0DTE; intraday amplification is contested.
- "Circuit breakers prevent crashes": they pause the tape (1997, 2020); in 2015 LULD itself created ETF dislocations; the reopening is the risk.
- "One spoofer caused the flash crash": the SEC/CFTC attributed it to a large sell algorithm interacting with HFT inventory limits.
- "2022 was low vol": RV was 24.2% (peak 34.3%), high by any standard; VIX simply never exceeded RV by much because nobody needed puts.
- "VIX > 40 means buy": measured from the first close above 40, the only entry a trader can act on (Cboe; pre-2016 SPX secondary): 2008-09-29 (46.72, SPX 1106.42) -> a further -39% to 676.53 (2009-03-09); 2010-05-07 (40.95, SPX 1110.88) -> -8% more to 1022.58 (07-02); 2011-08-08 (48.00, SPX 1119.46) -> -2% more with 4-5% daily whipsaws for two months; 2015-08-24 (40.74, SPX 1893.21) -> bottom the next day, retest -3% lower in Feb 2016; 2020-02-28 (40.11, SPX 2954.22) -> -24% more in 16 sessions while VIX doubled; 2025-04-04 (45.31, SPX 5074.08) -> -2% more, bottom in two sessions. A regime marker: 1-3 month returns are positive on average but the path can include 10-40% more drawdown; tradeable only with a second leg (long gamma or staged entries), never as a one-shot long.
- "-10% in two days means a crash is under way": Apr 2025 reversed on day four; continuation depends on the funding channel, not the drop size.
- "Short vol is free money": the VRP is real on average (Dew-Becker et al. 2017; Cheng 2019) but pays for convex losses that arrive in one week (LTCM, LJM, Malachite, Structured Alpha).
- "The Fed put was born in 2019" / "buy-the-dip began with Brexit": folklore; the Fed-put pattern is documented from the mid-1990s and dip-buying across the whole 2009-19 bull.
- Pitfalls: back-testing on Cboe VIX history before 2003 (back-filled); using the Cboe OPEN field as a true open (221 of 4,758 days since 2008 equal the prior close); counting Cboe's holiday/global-trading-hours rows (20 since 2022-07-04, e.g. 2025-06-19) as sessions; confusing the 2008-11-21 high (80.74) with the record close (80.86 on 11-20); quoting Feb 2018 as +104% instead of Cboe's +115.6%; citing FRED SP500 for pre-2016 closes (a trailing ten-year window); mixing Brent futures with Dated Brent spot in 2026 ($10-20 apart); quoting a "largest moves" list without saying whether back-filled pre-2003 rows are included.

## Contested or uncertain claims
- **1987 cause**: Brady Commission blamed portfolio insurance; Roll (1988) showed the crash was global, including markets without it (amplifier, not cause).
- **Feb 2018 causality**: ETP rebalancing (~$7-8bn of futures demand) vs discretionary/systematic short-vol unwinds; BIS and most practitioners centre the ETPs ("a key factor"), some argue they were too small to double the front futures alone. The XIV timetable, SVXY/UVXY leverage-change date and 200-300k-contract estimate remain unverified.
- **VIX settlement**: Griffin & Shams (2018, RFS) find settlement-morning volume and price patterns consistent with manipulation of the SOQ; Cboe attributes them to hedging of expiring positions; 2017-18 CFTC/Cboe inquiries produced no public enforcement. The 2018-04-18 settlement gap is reported, not recomputed [uncertain].
- **Dealer gamma as an index driver**: sell-side/vendor net-gamma estimates attribute intraday reversals to dealers; academics find support in single names and intraday momentum but index-level figures rest on assumed customer direction and can disagree on sign.
- **0DTE and intraday vol**: JPMorgan (2023) warned of a cascade; Cboe data show balanced flow; Brogaard-Han-Won (2023) find reduced vol in some samples, others find higher intraday vol; Beckmeyer-Branger-Gayda (2023) find retail 0DTE buyers lose on average. No consensus on crash amplification.
- **Was 2024-08-05's 65.73 "real"**: Cboe says computed per methodology; most practitioners treat it as a quote artifact; any subsequent methodology revision is unverified [uncertain].
- **2022 skew collapse**: structural (growing systematic put-selling/overwriting supply, post-2020 hedge fatigue) vs cyclical (light positioning); SKEW's record low near the 2022 bottom and record high (183.12) one day before the 2025 top support cyclical, while overwriting supply keeps growing.
- **GME gamma squeeze**: practitioners/FT describe one; SEC staff "did not find evidence of a gamma squeeze" and credit sentiment over short covering (resolved from the report text, 2026-10-14).
- **Tail-hedge P&L**: one review called "tail hedges paid in 2018/2020/2024/2025" folklore; PPUT beat SPX in 2008, 2018 and 2020 and lagged in every other year 2015-2025, so both "always pays in crises" and "never pays" are wrong (resolved with Cboe PPUT, 2026-10-14).
- **VRP sign in slow bears**: the earlier claim that it flips only in fast crashes was wrong; 2022 trailing RV exceeded VIX on a third of sessions (resolved 2026-10-14).
- **April 2025 Treasury selloff**: basis-trade unwind vs foreign official selling vs swap-spread trades; the pause was reportedly influenced by the bond market [uncertain].
- **Fed put**: whether rapid reversals (2019, 2020, 2025) have structurally lowered the frequency of VIX > 40 regimes or merely delayed them.
- **Vol-target AUM**: estimates of vol-control/risk-parity/CTA equity exposure range from hundreds of billions to > $1tn; the supply-chain sizes above are order-of-magnitude only.
- **2026 attributions**: the causes of 2026-06-05 and 2026-01-20 are not established (one review doubted any early-June US strikes; Wikipedia lists coastal-radar strikes on 06-06 and 06-09 and renewed strikes 06-26/27, all after the 06-05 close, plus the 4.2% CPI release on 06-10). Secondary 2026 Brent figures are front-month futures, $10-20 below FRED Dated Brent at the peaks. Whether Cboe's holiday rows (2025-06-19 etc.) are global-trading-hours computations is inferred from the NYSE calendar, not documented by Cboe.
