## Core concepts

- **OCC (Options Clearing Corporation)**: sole clearinghouse for all US listed options; its monthly "Volume Data" release is the canonical industry-wide count (contracts cleared, split equity / ETF / index, plus a small futures line). "Total options" below means OCC-cleared listed options unless stated.
- **ADV (average daily volume)**: contracts per trading day; OCC's "YTD ADV" = YTD contracts / YTD trading days (~21/month, ~251/year).
- **Multi-listed (multi-list) options**: options on stocks/ETFs that trade fungibly on every options exchange (SPY, NVDA, IBIT...). **Proprietary index options**: single-listed, exchange-exclusive products (Cboe's SPX, XSP, VIX, RUT, MGTN; Nasdaq's NDX). Cboe's "index options" line is proprietary only.
- **0DTE (zero days to expiration)**: a contract traded on its expiration day. SPX has had expirations every weekday since Tue/Thu were added in May 2022; SPY, QQQ, IWM and XSP/NDX also expire daily. "0DTE share" = 0DTE volume / total volume in that product.
- **SPX settlement conventions**: third-Friday SPX options are A.M.-settled (SET from opening prices; last trade the prior day); all other expirations (SPXW Mon-Fri, end-of-month) are P.M.-settled on the 4:00 p.m. close, so "0DTE" on a third Friday is the P.M.-settled SPXW series. VIX options/futures settle Wednesday mornings via the SOQ. Gamma therefore concentrates in the 3:00-4:00 p.m. window on P.M. days and at the 9:30 a.m. auction on A.M. days [stable Cboe contract specs; not re-fetched].
- **GTH (Global Trading Hours)**: Cboe's overnight session for SPX, XSP, VIX, RUT, MGTN and related products, 8:15 p.m. to 9:25 a.m. ET, Sunday night to Friday morning; "Curb" is the 4:15-5:00 p.m. ET post-close session. GTH dates to 2015 (3:00-9:15 a.m. ET) and became nearly 24x5 on 2021-11-21 [training; Cboe page 404 this session, uncertain]. Multi-list options have no overnight session (approved-but-unlaunched early/late sessions: see New products).
- **23x5 / near-24x5 equities trading**: SEC-approved overnight sessions on five exchanges (24X, Cboe EDGX, MEMX, Nasdaq, NYSE Arca): 9:00 p.m.-4:00 a.m. ET Sunday through Thursday nights, after a mandatory 8-9 p.m. pause (the 8 p.m.-4 a.m. window is Blue Ocean ATS, not the exchanges); limit orders only; SIPs run 9 p.m. Sunday to 8 p.m. Friday; target launch 2026-12-06 (WilmerHale 2026-09-29).
- **FLEX options**: exchange-listed contracts with customizable strike/expiry/exercise style, cleared by OCC; a fast-growing institutional channel (structured products, defined-outcome ETFs).
- **Binary / event contracts**: pay a fixed amount if a condition is met. CFTC-regulated versions (Kalshi, Polymarket US, Robinhood's venue, Crypto.com) are "event contracts" on DCMs. Cboe's "Cboe Predicts" binaries are SEC-regulated listed options: binary index options (XSP, Magnificent 10) clear at OCC under Cboe Rule 4.70; the KPI binaries (Cboe/Robinhood, settling on an issuer-reported KPI such as revenue rather than on the stock price) are intended to clear through Cboe Clear U.S. (CCUS), not yet a registered clearing agency (terms and status in New products and Rules).
- **Position limit / exercise limit**: max same-side contracts one account (or persons acting in concert) may hold, aggregated across exchanges; standard top tier 250,000 for the largest multi-list names. Cboe Rule 8.30 exceptions (C1 rulebook, Oct 2026): SPY 3,600,000; QQQ 1,800,000; IWM, EEM, FXI, EFA and (from 2026) IBIT 1,000,000; EWZ, TLT, EWJ, HYG, LQD, XLF, GDX 500,000; DIA 300,000. Proprietary broad-based index options (SPX, XSP, VIX, RUT) carry no position limits; binary KPI options 25,000 standard contracts per expiration (market-maker exemption); binary index options are limited per expiration (Rel. 34-105936).
- **Qualifying Securities (Nasdaq ISE, 2026)**: single stocks (>$700bn market cap) or ETFs (>$50bn AUM) with >10m monthly option sides, >=250,000 position limit and penny-interval membership; eligible for Monday and Wednesday short-term expirations in addition to Fridays, except on after-close earnings days.
- **Intraday margin standard (FINRA Rule 4210, 2026)**: replaced the pattern-day-trader (PDT) regime; firms must address intraday margin deficits whether or not a customer "day trades."
- **Reg NMS 2024 amendments**: $0.005 tick for tight-spread stocks, 10-mil access-fee cap, price-tiered round lots; adopted 2024-09-18. Compliance deferred twice by SEC exemptive orders, now to the first business day of November 2027; NOT in force as of 2026-10-14 (details in Rules).
- **Section 1256 treatment**: broad-based index options (SPX, XSP, NDX, RUT, VIX) are "nonequity options" taxed 60% long-term / 40% short-term and marked to market at year-end for US taxpayers; SPY/QQQ options are taxed at short-term rates. With European exercise and cash settlement (no early assignment, no share delivery), this is the main structural reason US retail has migrated toward XSP/SPX (XSP ADV +80% YoY in Q2 2026); it does not apply to non-US accounts [tax law settled; attribution is practitioner consensus, not measured].
- **VIX / VIX3M**: 30-day and 93-day SPX implied vol indices; VIX3M minus VIX > 0 indicates contango (normal), < 0 backwardation (stress).

## Mechanisms

- **Why volume keeps compounding (2020-2026)**: (1) daily expirations turned SPX/SPY/QQQ into intraday instruments, so one directional view can be expressed many times per week; (2) zero-commission retail brokers (Robinhood, Schwab, IBKR, Webull) monetize via payment for order flow (PFOF) and exchange rebates, so they market options aggressively; (3) smaller notional products (XSP = 1/10 SPX; Mini/Nano products; $1 strikes) lower the ticket size, so contract counts rise faster than notional; (4) exchanges compete on listings (more expirations, more symbols, longer hours) because each new listing adds fee-bearing volume at near-zero marginal cost; (5) institutions use FLEX/blocks for defined-outcome ETFs and overlay strategies, adding a steady institutional base.
- **Who the actors are and what they want**: exchanges (Cboe, Nasdaq, NYSE, MIAX, MEMX, BOX, IEX) want volume and proprietary products (SPX licensing is why Cboe locked the S&P DJI deal to 2051); retail brokers want engagement and PFOF; wholesalers/market makers (Citadel Securities, Susquehanna, IMC, Optiver, Wolverine, Jane Street [general industry knowledge, no 2026 share data]) want flow they can internalize or route to exchanges with favorable rebates; the OCC wants margin adequacy; the SEC/FINRA want to show they are responding to retail-risk and extended-hours integrity concerns without blocking product innovation; the CFTC under Chair Michael Selig wants federal preemption for event contracts; states want gambling jurisdiction and tax revenue.
- **Exchange economics**: options venues run maker-taker (rebates to posted liquidity) or classic pro-rata/customer-priority fee models [venue assignment not verified]; retail flow executes mostly through price-improvement auctions (Cboe AIM, Nasdaq PIM, MIAX PRIME, NYSE CUBE) in which the wholesaler guarantees a price and the crowd competes to improve it; OCC clearing fees are a few cents per contract [2026 level not verified]. The Reg NMS 10-mil cap is an equities change; options fee schedules and tick programs are unaffected.
- **Why 0DTE share keeps rising even in calm markets**: the per-day gamma payoff is cheapest in absolute premium; low VIX makes 0DTE lottery tickets cheap and short-premium strategies (iron condors, credit spreads) attractive to income sellers; brokers and content creators promote both sides; the product set keeps expanding (XSP 0DTE, Mon/Wed single-stock expirations). SPX series: ~22% when Tue/Thu expirations launched (May 2022), 57% Q3 2025, 59% full-year 2025, ~61% of Q2-2026 volume (3.1m/5.1m), 65% monthly in May 2026, record 66.2% July 2026, ~65% Q3 2026 (3.2m/4.9m) (Cboe).
- **Why dealer gamma matters and why evidence says the net effect is usually small**: market makers who are net short gamma must buy rallies and sell dips to stay delta-neutral (destabilizing); net long gamma does the opposite. Cboe's proprietary trade data (Amaya, Garcia-Ares, Pearson, Vasquez 2025) show aggregate OMM gamma in SPX 0DTE is usually positive with a bounded worst case (+3.3 vol points on daily realized vol, +6.4 on 30-minute windows). Regime caveat: the sample is SPX/SPXW trades Jul 2020-Jun 2023 (0DTE share then ~20-45%, ADV well under today's 3m+); mean impact -0.19 vol pts daily (-0.17 on 30-min windows), median -0.08, amplifying on a minority of days and ~10% of 30-minute windows; not re-estimated on 2024-26 data, so treat +3.3/+6.4 as a lower bound on today's worst case. Cboe's "0DTEs Decoded" (May 2025) attributes the small average effect to balanced put/call and buy/sell flow. Counter-argument: averages hide tail days when positioning becomes one-sided (e.g., 2024-08-05, 2025-04-07).
- **Why vol spikes in 2025-2026 reverted quickly**: three shocks (tariffs April 2025; China-tariff threat 2025-10-10; US/Israel-Iran war March 2026) were policy/geopolitical rather than credit/funding events; each saw record or near-record options volume as hedgers and 0DTE traders piled in, then systematic vol sellers (put-write, covered-call ETFs, dispersion desks) and retail premium sellers compressed implied vol within weeks. OptionMetrics: April 2025 took 5 days to peak and 14 days to revert. Sizing the systematic supply: no 2026 AUM for covered-call ETFs (JEPI/JEPQ family), buffer/defined-outcome ETFs (main FLEX users) or put-write strategies was verified [uncertain]; FLEX ADV (1.4m in 2025, ~2.8m in Q2 2026) is the only verified proxy.
- **Implied correlation and dispersion**: with SPX = 81% of index volume and NVDA/TSLA = 9% each of single-stock volume, index-vol selling (overlay ETFs, 0DTE sellers) against single-name vol buying makes implied correlation (Cboe COR1M/COR3M) and the S&P 500 Dispersion Index (DSPX, launched 2023 [training]) the dominant 2024-26 index-vol theme; a dispersion trade sells index vol and buys constituent vol, profiting when realized correlation falls below implied. Cboe flags variance and dispersion futures as growth areas [no COR1M/DSPX level verified; dispersion-futures status uncertain].
- **Why 23x5 matters for options**: overnight cash-equity trading already exists on ATSs (Blue Ocean, Bruce, IBKR Eos, MOON; ~0.9% of NMS share volume as of Aug 2026, +359% YoY) and SPX dealers already hedge GTH exposure in ES futures (CME runs ~23 hours). From 2026-12-06 overnight trading moves onto exchanges with a consolidated tape, LULD overnight bands and clearly-erroneous rules (details in Rules). Consequences: (a) an official overnight NBBO for the first time, which weakens the market-structure argument against overnight multi-list options; (b) better cash-hedge liquidity and price references for GTH SPX and any future overnight single-name sessions; (c) more of the overnight move prints on-exchange, which matters for 1DTE positions carried overnight and for opening-auction gamma.
- **Why prediction markets are now part of the options landscape**: event contracts are economically binary options; Robinhood sells them beside options (13.6bn event contracts in Q2 2026 [search-summary figure; uncertain]); Cboe lists SEC-regulated binaries on XSP and, once CCUS is live, on company KPIs. Regulation is split between CFTC (DCM event contracts) and SEC (listed binaries), with states contesting sports contracts in court.
- **Why Fed policy shifted to hiking in 2026**: WTI (FRED): $56.01 (Jan 7 low) -> $71.13 (Mar 2) -> $114.58 (Apr 7 high) -> $96.17 (Apr 8, -16% in one session on de-escalation; VIX 25.78 -> 21.04) -> $69.60 (Jul 6 trough) -> $85-100 in late Sept/early Oct ($99.37 Sep 28; $96.24 Oct 6). Headline inflation re-accelerated; the FOMC held at 3.50-3.75% through July (9-3, three hawkish dissents) and hiked 25 bp on 2026-09-16 (12-0) to 3.75-4.00%, the first hike since 2023, after the Q3 oil re-rally and the 10y's break above 5.00% on 2026-09-15 (first 5-handle close since July 2007; peak 5.31% 2026-10-05; 5.24% 2026-10-09; 30y 5.60%). High long yields support elevated equity put skew via the equity-rates correlation channel [skew level not verified].

## Formulas and quantitative rules

```
Annualized run-rate from OCC YTD ADV:
  annual_contracts ~= YTD_ADV * 251
  2026: 70.565m * 251 ~= 17.7bn (Cboe's own 2026 estimate: "well above 18bn")

2025 growth reconciliation (two numbers circulate):
  contracts: 15.207bn / 12.224bn - 1 = +24.4%   [2025 OCC total uncertain]
  ADV:       ~61.0m / 48.5m - 1 = +25.8%        (250 trading days in 2025 vs 252 in 2024)
  Cboe's "26%" matches the ADV computation; do not quote it as contract growth.

0DTE share:
  share_0DTE = ADV_0DTE / ADV_product
  SPX Q2 2026: 3.1m / 5.1m = 0.61;  SPX Q3 2026: 3.2m / 4.9m = 0.65 (July 2026 monthly record 66.2%)

Implied 1-day move from VIX (rule of 16):
  sigma_daily ~= VIX / sqrt(252) ~= VIX / 15.87
  VIX 15.27 (2026-10-13 close) -> ~0.96% expected daily 1-sigma move;  VIX 31.05 (2026-03-27) -> ~1.96%

Term structure slope (contango if positive):
  slope_1m3m = VIX3M - VIX = 17.96 - 15.27 = +2.69 vol pts (2026-10-13)  -> normal contango

S&P 500 from FRED SP500:
  YTD 2026 = 7,805.45 / 6,845.50 - 1 = +14.0% (2026-10-13);  March 2026 = 6,528.52 / 6,878.88 - 1 = -5.1%

Dealer hedge-rebalance flow (what the gamma debate quantifies):
  dHedge_shares = -Gamma_OMM_total * dS,  Gamma_OMM_total = sum over OMM positions (contract gamma * multiplier * contracts, signed)
  If Gamma_OMM_total < 0: OMMs buy when S rises, sell when S falls (amplifying).
  Amaya et al. (2025), sample Jul 2020-Jun 2023: max daily impact +3.3 pts; mean -0.19; median -0.08 (dampening).

Event-contract / binary pricing:
  price (0-1) ~= risk-neutral probability; payout = $1 (Kalshi; Cboe KPI binaries) or contract-specific fixed amount.
  EV for a buyer at price p with subjective probability q: EV = q - p - fees.
  Cboe KPI binary: call pays $1 if reported KPI >= strike else $0; put pays $1 if KPI < strike;
  100 binary contracts = 1 standard contract for limits, so 25,000 standard = 2.5m binary contracts per expiration.

Overnight trade-date convention (23x5 equities, from 2026-12-06; CAT Trade Date = 8:00 p.m. ET prior day to 8:00 p.m. ET same day):
  trades from 8:00 p.m. ET onward -> trade date = next calendar day (settle T+1 from that date)
  trades 12:00-4:00 a.m. ET -> trade date = that day; weekends/holidays are not trade dates

Position-limit sanity (IBIT, 2026):
  1,000,000 contracts * 100 shares = 100m shares ~= 7.5% of 1,337,920,000 shares outstanding (Arca filing: "exercisable risk" ~7.47%)
```

## Practitioner heuristics

- **Read OCC on the 1st-2nd business day, Cboe on the ~5th**: OCC gives the market-wide picture; Cboe gives SPX/0DTE/GTH. OCC segment prints are noisy month to month (Aug 2026 equity +1.9% YoY, Sept -13.9%, YTD +9-13%); read the YTD ADV columns, not single months. The durable 2026 pattern is ETF options growing ~35% YTD vs single-stock ~10%, driven by SPY/QQQ daily expirations, IBIT after the limit increase and leveraged single-stock ETF options [driver attribution uncertain]. Do not infer anything about single-name vol-of-vol from the volume mix without checking single-name implied-vol data directly.
- **SPX volume concentrates at 0DTE (~65%) and the monthly/quarterly cycle**: before assuming non-standard mid-dated expirations are thin, check OCC open interest by expiry and Cboe book depth; this file has no liquidity-by-DTE data.
- **Record volume days coincide with sharp one-day moves, not necessarily high VIX**: 2025-04-04 (101.9m, VIX 45.31) vs 2025-10-10 (110m, VIX close 21.66, 19.03 the next session). Treat a volume record as 0DTE/hedging intensity on the day, not evidence of a vol-regime change; check the term structure and realized vol over the following 3-5 sessions. The three largest 2025-26 spikes reverted within 3-6 weeks; this breaks if the shock is credit/funding rather than policy (2008, Mar 2020).
- **VIX 15 with VIX3M 18 and 10y >5%**: classic "carry on, but don't sell the tail" setup. Rule of 16: 1-sigma daily move ~1%; with the S&P at records and long yields at multi-decade highs, the rates-equity correlation can flip quickly, so prefer put spreads to naked short puts.
- **PDT repeal (2026-06-04)**: Cboe reports activity in accounts <$25k rose in Q2 2026; the only prior small-lot statistic is MEMX's 30.2% of volume in trades <=10 contracts (Aug 2025, secondary). Hypothesis (untested): more sub-10-lot 0DTE prints and last-hour churn; verify with OPRA trade-size distributions before using in sizing or signals. Regardless, size against notional/premium, not contract count.
- **GTH SPX is ~4% of SPX volume**: overnight markets are quotable but wide; use GTH for event hedges (Asia/Europe shocks) and expect to pay 2-5x daytime spread [practitioner estimate, uncertain]. After the 23x5 launch (2026-12-06), re-check GTH spreads: dealers will have an on-exchange cash hedge and an official overnight NBBO.
- **Position-limit changes lead institutional adoption**: IBIT's 25k -> 250k -> 1m path mirrors the IWM/EEM tier; expect more IBIT FLEX/block and overwriting activity. The IBIT-vs-Deribit skew gap is driven by hours (6.5h vs 24/7), settlement (shares vs BTC/USD), margin and tax, not limits [hypothesis].
- **Earnings-week expiry map for Qualifying Securities**: Mon/Wed expiries are skipped on after-close earnings days, so the post-earnings 0DTE is the Friday series; check ISE's quarterly eligibility list on the first trading day of each quarter before assuming a name still has Mon/Wed listings.
- **Prediction-market prices are usable event-risk inputs but notional-light vs options**: ~$50bn/month across all event contracts vs trillions of SPX notional; for Fed/CPI binaries, compare the event-contract probability with the options-implied distribution (SPX straddle) rather than trusting either alone.
- **Regulatory calendar matters to vol**: 2026-10-27/28 FOMC; late-Oct 2026 earliest Cboe single-name extended-hours launch; KPI binaries once CCUS is registered; 2026-12-06 23x5 launch; Nov 2027 Reg NMS tick/fee-cap compliance; SEBI weekly-expiry decision [uncertain]. India is the only regulator-grade natural experiment (figures in New products): four measures landed at once, participation fell 20%, losses fell ~18%, yet 59% of index-option turnover still occurred on expiry day; attribution is not separable, so read it as evidence that cutting expiries/raising ticket size cuts participation more than it cuts expiry-day concentration.

## Common misconceptions and pitfalls

- "OCC total options ADV" and "Cboe options ADV" are different denominators: Cboe's 21.9m (Q2 2026) is Cboe-only; the industry was 72.8m.
- 0DTE share figures differ by definition: Cboe's "0DTE" means traded on expiration day; vendors sometimes count 0-1 DTE. July 2026's 66.2% and Q3's derived ~65% are consistent, but a vendor's "63% in Feb 2026" is a different measure. Q2 2026 is ~61% for the quarter; 65% is the May monthly figure.
- "2025 volume grew 26%" is ADV growth; contract growth was ~24%.
- The $0.005 tick and 10-mil fee cap are equities changes and are not yet in force (deferred to November 2027); options ticks (penny/nickel programs) did not change in 2025-2026.
- 23x5 equities trading does not mean 23x5 options trading; multi-list options still close at 4:00/4:15 p.m. ET. Cboe's single-name early/late sessions are approved but not live as of 2026-10-14; GTH proprietary products are the only overnight listed options.
- The IBIT position limit increase is per exchange rule but limits are applied across all exchanges; the 1m figure is same-side, not gross.
- "Cboe Predicts is cleared by Cboe Clear U.S." is premature: binary index options clear at OCC; CCUS is an applicant, not a registered clearing agency.
- Retail "50-60% of volume" refers to retail-broker-routed contracts, not notional or premium; by premium, institutions dominate (top-50 products = 85% of premium).
- Record "options volume" in 2025 (15.2bn) vs "record cleared volume" (15.26bn): the latter includes OCC-cleared futures.
- Prediction-market "volume" is notional taker volume; a $1 contract trading at $0.95 counts $0.95, and wash/round-trip activity inflates sports figures; not comparable to options contract counts.
- The 2026-03-27 VIX high (31.05 close) is far below April 2025 (52.33); do not describe the Iran shock as comparable in vol terms to the tariff shock despite the larger oil move.
- Fed "first hike since 2023" (2026-09-16) came after three 2025 cuts; the policy rate (3.75-4.00%) is still below the 2024 peak (5.25-5.50%).

## Contested or uncertain claims

- **0DTE systemic risk**: Cboe-sponsored research (Amaya et al. 2025; Cboe "0DTEs Decoded" May 2025) and Dim, Eraker and Vilkov (2024, SSRN 4692190) find net dealer gamma is usually positive/balanced and 0DTE does not amplify intraday vol on average (bounded worst case +3.3 daily vol points on 2020-23 data). Critics (sell-side strategists, some risk managers, FINRA in its 4210 rationale) argue averages hide tail days and that 46%/yr 0DTE growth plus smaller accounts raises intraday fragility. No 2026 Fed/BIS paper settling this was found. Status: contested; evidence favors "small average effect, non-zero tail effect", with the caveat that the bound predates the 2024-26 regime.
- **Retail losses**: SEBI's FY26 data (87.7% of individual F&O traders lose; options = 92% of losses) is the only regulator-grade loss statistic found; no US regulator publishes an equivalent. Academic US estimates (e.g., Beckmeyer, Branger and Gayda on retail 0DTE; Bryzgalova, Pavlova and Sikorskaya on retail options) exist but titles/years were not re-verified [uncertain]. Whether US retail 0DTE P&L is negative in aggregate is widely assumed, not verified here.
- **Retail share (43-48% vs 50-60%)**: depends on whether "retail" = all 606-reported retail-broker flow (upper) or only small-lot trades (lower). Cboe's language has drifted from "50-60%" to "nearly half."
- **Vol suppression**: the claim that systematic option-selling structurally caps VIX is supported by fast reversions in 2025-26 and a 2026 mean VIX of ~17.9 despite a war and a hiking Fed, but not proven and not sized (no overlay-ETF AUM verified); counter-evidence is the April 2025 spike to 52 and the 35.30 intraday 52-week high in 2026.
- **Dealer concentration**: widely asserted that two wholesalers handle most retail options flow; no public 2026 share data verified. Rule 606 reports give venue-level routing, not market-maker share.
- **2025 segment growth**: OCC-derived (equity +26.8%, ETF +21.9%, index +20.3%; unverifiable this session) vs Cboe's 2025 review (single-stock +28%, ETF +32%, index +21%); both cannot be right on OCC's 2024 base. Resolution pending re-fetch of the OCC release.
- **Resolved during review** (no longer contested): Reg NMS deferral to Nov 2027; KPI approval 2026-10-13; Minnesota injunction 2026-07-27; Cboe single-name extended hours (July target missed; live status still unverified); the "nine states" CFTC-suit list (supported by RotoWire's 2026-06-24 entry, contrary to one reviewer's doubt); Warsh votes (51-45 member, 54-45 chair).
