## 1. Contract mechanics and settlement

- **0DTE** (zero days to expiration) — any option traded on its expiration day; SPX (SPXW) has had a PM-settled expiry every weekday since the Tuesday/Thursday listings of April-May 2022; 0DTE share of SPX volume 59% FY2025 and a record 66.2% in July 2026 (owner 11). → 01, 11, 03, 05, 07, 08, 09, 13
- **Adjusted contract** (non-standard deliverable, "1"-suffix root, e.g. SPY1) — option whose deliverable, strike or multiplier was changed by OCC after a split, spin-off, merger or special dividend >= $12.50 per contract ($0.125/share, policy since 2009-02-01); far less liquid, can strand a leg. → 01, 09, 12
- **AM settlement** (AM-settled; SET, NDS, RLS, VRO) — settlement value computed from each index component's opening print on expiration morning; last trading day is the prior business day (SPX monthlies stop Thursday 5:00 pm ET at the end of Curb). SPX third-Friday = SET, NDX = NDS, RUT = RLS, VIX = VRO. → 01, 05, 09, 12, 13
- **American exercise** — exercisable any business day through expiration; all US listed equity/ETF options (SPY, QQQ, IWM) and OEX; a short American option can be assigned any night. → 01, 02, 07
- **Assignment** — OCC's allocation of an exercise notice to a short position: holder -> broker -> clearing member -> OCC random selection among clearing members overnight -> broker allocation (random or FIFO); the writer learns next morning. → 01, 07, 13
- **Call / put / series / class** — call = right to buy the deliverable at strike K on/before expiration; put = right to sell; payoffs max(S_T - K, 0) and max(K - S_T, 0); the writer carries the mirror-image obligation.; series = one root + expiration + strike + type, class = all series on one underlying (~2 million listed strikes, about half with zero OI, SEC roundtable 2026-04-16) → 01, 02, 09
- **Cash settlement / physical settlement** — payout (settlement value - K) x multiplier in cash, moving next business day; SPX, SPXW, XSP, VIX, NDX, RUT, Nanos; no pin risk in the deliverable but settlement-value risk.; physical settlement = shares change hands, the stock leg settling T+1 (since 2024-05-28); all US equity/ETF options → 01, 09, 11
- **Contrary Exercise Advice** (CEA; Expiring Exercise Declaration, EED; do-not-exercise, DNE) — instruction overriding exercise-by-exception (exercise an OTM option or decline an ITM one); exists for equity/ETF options and standard third-Friday index monthlies only; nonstandard index expirations (SPXW, XSP weeklies) exercise automatically with no contrary instruction (OCC Rule 1804(c); SEC approval Rel. 34-95364 of 2022-07-26). → 01, 09
- **Curb session** — Cboe post-close session 4:15-5:00 pm ET for SPX/SPXW/XSP/VIX/RUT; 4:00-4:15 pm ET for designated multi-list classes (Cboe Rule 5.1(d)); OCC classifies Curb as RTH for risk purposes. → 01, 09, 11, 12
- **Daily expirations / Weeklys / EOM / Quarterlys** — non-third-Friday series; Mon-Fri expirations on SPXW, DJXW (SEC approval 2026-04-10), SPY/QQQ (Tue/Thu from 2022-11), IWM (2023 [uncertain date]); Nasdaq ISE Mon/Wed single-stock expirations for Qualifying Securities (2026-01-16).; EOM = last business day of the month (SPXW, XSP), Quarterlys = last business day of Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec (SPXW), both PM-settled → 01, 09, 11
- **Dividend risk / dividend assignment / dividend play** — an ITM call writer assigned the night before ex-date is short stock on ex-date and owes the dividend; exercise is rational when D > same-strike put value + K(1 - e^{-r tau}); SPY ex-dates fall on the third Friday of Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec, so ITM SPY calls in that expiry are routinely assigned Thursday night.; dividend play = market makers trade large offsetting deep-ITM calls on the last cum-dividend day and exercise all longs, capturing unexercised public dividends and inflating reported volume → 01, 07, 03, 08, 12, 13
- **Early-exercise premium** (EEP) — American minus European value; driven by dividends and borrow (calls) and interest on the strike (puts); CRR example S=K=100, 20% vol, r=5%, 1y: American put 6.089 vs European 5.570 (EEP ~9% of the put); near zero in zero-rate regimes. → 02, 01
- **ES / MES options** (/ES; E-mini and Micro E-mini S&P 500 options on futures, CME) — ES option multiplier $50 per index point (future tick 0.25 pt = $12.50), MES $5; quarterly options American-style expiring with the future to the SOQ; weekly (Mon-Fri) and EOM options reported European-style settling 3:00 pm CT into the nearest quarterly future [uncertain; CME pages unreachable 2026-10-14]; priced with Black-76; margined under SPAN; Section 1256. → 01, 02, 07, 09
- **European exercise** — exercisable only at expiration (in practice automatic cash settlement); SPX, SPXW, XSP, VIX, NDX, RUT, Nanos, XEO; cash-settled index options are European because an American cash-settled option creates a wildcard option. → 01, 02
- **Exercise by exception** (ex-by-ex) — OCC's automatic exercise of expiring options absent contrary instructions: equity options ITM by >= $0.01 per share against the primary-market 4:00 pm last sale (OCC Rule 805, threshold cut from $0.05 to $0.01 in 2008); index options with Exercise Settlement Amount >= $1.00 per contract (Rule 1804), Nanos $0.01 per contract.; Exercise Settlement Amount = |settlement value - K| x multiplier → 01, 09, 03, 12, 13
- **Exercise cut-off** — customer final exercise decision close+1h30 = 5:30 pm ET (2:30 pm on a 1:00 pm early close; FINRA 2360(b)(23)(A)(iii)); CEA / Advice Cancel to the exchange: customer accounts by close+3h30 (7:30 pm ET); non-customer (firm proprietary) accounts by 7:30 pm only via an electronic time-stamped process, else close+1h30 (FINRA 2360(b)(23)(A)(iv)); brokers set earlier deadlines (often 4:00-5:30 pm ET); stock trades to 8:00 pm, so post-close moves create after-hours assignment risk. → 01, 09, 07, 12, 13
- **Extrinsic (time) value / intrinsic value** — intrinsic = value of immediate exercise (call max(S - K, 0), put max(K - S, 0)); extrinsic = premium minus intrinsic; bundles volatility value and carry (interest on K, dividends, borrow); >= 0 for American options, can be negative for a European option measured against spot intrinsic (deep-ITM European put bounded by K e^{-rT} - S); ATM time value ~0.4 S sigma sqrt(T). → 01, 02, 07
- **FLEX options** — exchange-listed, OCC-cleared contracts with negotiable strike, expiry (any business day up to 15 years), exercise style and settlement (Cboe Rules 4.20-4.22); the vehicle for buffer ETFs and structured-note hedges; FLEX ADV ~2.8M = 3.8% of industry volume in Q2 2026. → 01, 09, 11, 05
- **GTH** (Global Trading Hours) — Cboe overnight session 8:15 pm-9:25 am ET (orders from 8:00 pm) for SPX/SPXW, XSP, VIX and, since 2026-02-12, RUT/RUTW; the SPX cash index is not computed, so dealers hedge and researchers proxy the underlying with ES; GTH ADV 263K in Sept 2026. → 01, 09, 11, 12, 13
- **Hard-to-borrow** (HTB) — stock with high stock-loan fees; drives early exercise of deep-ITM calls held against short stock (exercise iff S x borrow_rate x tau > extrinsic), makes puts trade rich via parity and makes American boxes on HTB names unsafe. → 01, 02, 07
- **Holiday expiration shift** — Tue-Fri and third-Friday expirations falling on a holiday move to the prior business day, Monday weeklies to the next; a Good Friday third Friday makes AM-settled monthlies last-trade Wednesday with SET computed Thursday; Juneteenth 2026 moved the June expiration to Thu June 18 and the May VIX settlement to Tue May 19 [dates derived]. → 01, 05
- **LEAPS** — long-term series expiring 12 to 180 months from listing (Cboe Rules 4.5(f), 4.13(b)); in practice chains run ~2-3 years for equities/ETFs and ~5 years for SPX December LEAPS [uncertain]; Reg T loan value 75% for listed options over nine months. → 01, 07
- **Moneyness** (ITM / ATM / OTM) — call ITM if S > K, put ITM if S < K, ATM if S ~ K; measured against the forward, not spot: S/K, log-moneyness k = ln(K/F), standardized ln(K/F)/(sigma sqrt(T)), or delta (|delta| ~ 0.50 at ATM-forward, 25-delta = |delta| 0.25). → 01, 02, 03
- **Monthly (standard) expiration** — third Friday of the month (the Friday itself since Feb 2015, previously the following Saturday); SPX monthlies are AM-settled; PM-settled third-Friday index series may be listed under weekly roots since 2019-10-07 (Cboe Rule 4.13 Interp .13).; equity classes sit in one of three quarterly cycles (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct, Feb/May/Aug/Nov, Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec) with the two nearest months plus the next two cycle months always listed → 01
- **Multiplier / premium / notional** — contract size factor: 100 for equity/ETF options and $100 per index point for SPX, XSP, NDX, RUT, VIX, DJX; Nanos $1; ES options $50; MES options $5; VIX futures $1,000.; premium = the price the buyer pays the writer, quoted per share (equity/ETF) or per index point, dollar amount = premium x multiplier; notional = underlying level x multiplier (at an illustrative 6,500: SPX $650,000, XSP = SPY $65,000, ES option $325,000, MES $32,500, Nanos $650; 1 SPX ~ 10 SPY ~ 10 XSP ~ 2 ES ~ 20 MES ~ 1,000 Nanos) → 01, 02, 03, 13
- **Nanos** (NANOS; One Multiplier Options) — Cboe option on 1/100th of XSP (SPX/1000) with $1 multiplier, European, cash-settled, Mon/Wed/Fri expirations, $0.01 tick, auto-exercise at $0.01 per contract; launched 2022-03-14; effectively dormant (volume 88, OI 135 on 2026-10-13 [author-observed]). → 01, 09
- **OEX / XEO** — S&P 100 options: OEX is the American-style cash-settled exception (exercisable through 4:20 pm during a halt at or after 4:00 pm), XEO its European twin; VXO is the old OEX-based vol index.; OEX's wildcard option (value from exercising after the 4:00 pm index value is fixed but before the cut-off; Fleming-Whaley 1994) is why cash-settled index options moved to European style → 01, 10
- **Open interest** (OI) — contracts outstanding (one long and one short each), updated overnight by OCC from cleared positions: open vs open +1, close vs close -1, open vs close unchanged; every intraday figure is the prior close ("start-of-day"); OI has no side and understates 0DTE positioning. → 01, 12, 05
- **OPEX / witching** (monthly expiration; triple/quad witching) — OPEX = third-Friday monthly expiration; triple/quad witching = third Friday of Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec when index futures, AM index options, stock options and single-stock futures expire together and the S&P quarterly rebalance trades at the close; daily expirations have diluted monthly OPEX gamma. → 05, 01, 06, 09
- **OSI symbology** (Options Symbology Initiative, 2010) — 21-character symbol: 6-char space-padded root + YYMMDD + C/P + 8-digit strike x 1000; e.g. "SPXW  261218P06500000" = SPXW 2026-12-18 6500 put; adjusted classes carry a numeral in the root. → 01, 12
- **Pin risk** — at expiration the underlying sits at or within cents of a short strike and the writer cannot know until assignment notices arrive (after the exercise cut-off) whether they hold the underlying; after-hours moves can flip ITM/OTM; cash-settled index options have none. → 01, 03, 05, 07, 13
- **PM settlement** — settlement at the official index close on expiration day, built from constituent closing-auction prices; SPXW weekday/EOM/quarterly series, XSP, SPY/QQQ (physical); removes the Thursday-night gap but adds closing-auction basis; expiring SPXW/XSP stop trading at 4:00 pm ET (1:00 pm half days). → 01, 11, 12, 05
- **SET** — SPX AM exercise-settlement value: S&P 500 computed from each constituent's first regular-session trade on its primary market on expiration Friday; not the 9:30 index print; SET basis (SET minus the 9:30 index/futures print) is the unhedgeable residual, a few points normally and far more on 8:30 am macro-release or gap opens. → 01, 05, 07, 12, 13
- **SOQ / VRO** (Special Opening Quotation) — AM settlement value built from opening prints: for index options the constituent opens (SET/RLS/NDS), for VIX the opening trade prices (not mid-quotes) of the single SPX expiry 30 days out in the HOSS opening auction; SOQ can differ from surrounding spot prints by a point or more.; VRO is the VIX settlement ticker and can differ from Tuesday's VIX close by multiple points (Griffin-Shams 2018 read settlement-time volume spikes as manipulation; Cboe points to the HOSS auction rules, RG18-015) → 01, 04, 02, 06, 09, 10, 12
- **SPX / SPXW** — Cboe S&P 500 index options, $100 multiplier, European, cash-settled, no position limits, Section 1256; root SPX = AM-settled third-Friday monthlies (last trade Thursday 5:00 pm ET); root SPXW = PM-settled weekday, EOM and quarterly series; SPX ADV 3.9M in 2025, 4.9M in Q3 2026, 74% of S&P 500-linked option volume in 2025. → 01, 09, 11
- **SPY** (SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust) — unit investment trust (1993), full replication, no securities lending, dividends held without interest until quarterly distribution; options are American, physically settled, trade to 4:15 pm ET, position limit 3,600,000 contracts, taxed as equity options (not 1256); ~1/10 SPX notional. → 01, 06, 09, 07
- **VIX expiration** — Wednesday 30 days before the third Friday of the following month, so the SOQ's SPX options have exactly 30 days left; last trading day the preceding Tuesday (through Curb to 5:00 pm ET); settlement to VRO; cash paid next business day; weekly VIX settles to SPXW Friday weeklies only. → 01, 04, 05
- **XSP** (Mini-SPX) — Cboe option on 1/10 SPX, $100 multiplier, European, cash-settled, PM-settled, Section 1256, $1 strikes, quoted in $0.01 while SPY is in the Penny Program, aggregated 10:1 with SPX for (nonexistent) limits; marketed as the retail SPY substitute; ADV 274K in Sept 2026 (+80% YoY in Q2 2026). → 01, 09, 11

## 2. Pricing and models

- **Bachelier (normal) model** — arithmetic Brownian motion dF = sigma_N dW; prices quoted in a normal vol in price units; admits negative forwards and strikes; ATM price e^{-rT} sigma_N sqrt(T/(2 pi)) ~ 0.399 e^{-rT} sigma_N sqrt(T); CME's live convention for WTI-linked options since April 2020 (switch 2020-04-22 after the -$37.63 settlement [date uncertain]). → 02
- **Black-76** — BSM written on the forward/futures price F: C = e^{-rT}[F N(d1) - K N(d2)], d1 = [ln(F/K) + sigma^2 T/2]/(sigma sqrt(T)); standard for ES/MES options and VIX options (forward = the matching VIX future); futures-style margined options drop the discount factor.; futures-style margined options (Lieu 1990: premium settled through variation margin, never exercised early) use the undiscounted form, while premium-upfront options on futures such as ES can be exercised early → 02, 03
- **Black-Scholes-Merton** (BSM) — closed-form European price under geometric Brownian motion with constant sigma, r, q: C = S e^{-qT} N(d1) - K e^{-rT} N(d2), d1 = [ln(S/K) + (r - q + sigma^2/2)T]/(sigma sqrt(T)), d2 = d1 - sigma sqrt(T); benchmark S=K=100, r=5%, q=0, sigma=20%, T=1: C = 10.4506, P = 5.5735; eight assumptions listed in 02; used as a quoting map, not a description of dynamics. → 02, 03
- **Box spread** — long call spread plus short put spread at K1 < K2; European payoff exactly K2 - K1; price B = (K2 - K1) e^{-rT} reveals the options market's financing rate r = -ln(B/(K2 - K1))/T, typically tens of bp above Treasuries and close to SOFR/OIS (SPX boxes ~30-50 bp over matching Treasuries, rates as of 2025-02-24); never sell an American-style box (Robinhood 2019 incident); BOXX ETF (inception 2022-12-27) lends via boxes. → 02, 07, 12
- **Breeden-Litzenberger** (risk-neutral density, RND) — q(K) = e^{rT} d2C/dK2 (1978); the scaled butterfly spread [C(K - dK) - 2C(K) + C(K + dK)]/dK^2 is the discrete estimate; checks: integral = 1, E^Q[S_T] = F; negative densities signal butterfly arbitrage or noisy mids, so smooth in IV space (SVI) first. → 02
- **Brenner-Subrahmanyam approximation** — ATM-forward option ~ 0.4 F e^{-rT} sigma sqrt(T) (straddle ~ 0.8 F e^{-rT} sigma sqrt(T)); relative error ~ sigma^2 T/24, fine while sigma sqrt(T) <~ 0.5; inverted it seeds IV solvers: sigma ~ sqrt(2 pi/T) C/S. → 02, 01, 03, 04
- **Calendar arbitrage** — total implied variance w(k, T) = sigma^2 T must be non-decreasing in T at fixed log-moneyness k (requires proportional dividends); violation = negative forward variance; an event lump in the front expiry can invert calendar-time IV without being arbitrage. → 02, 04
- **Carr-Madan static replication** (1998) — any twice-differentiable European payoff f(S_T) equals a bond, a forward and a strip of OTM puts below F and calls above F weighted by f''(K); the log contract (f'' = -1/K^2) gives the variance-swap strike; digital = -dC/dK; also names the Carr-Madan FFT pricing method (damping alpha ~ 1-2). → 02, 03
- **CRR binomial tree** (Cox-Ross-Rubinstein 1979) — discrete lattice with u = e^{sigma sqrt(dt)}, d = 1/u, risk-neutral p = (e^{(r - q)dt} - d)/(u - d); American value = max(discounted continuation, intrinsic); converges to BSM with even/odd oscillation (use n >= 200-500 or Leisen-Reimer); OptionMetrics IVs come from a CRR tree with projected discrete dividends [not re-verified]. → 02, 12
- **De-Americanized IV** — implied vol backed out with an American pricer (lattice/BAW) rather than BSM for American-style quotes; vendor IVs on the same quote differ by ~0.5-1 vol point in ITM/short-dated/HTB cases [uncertain] and by several points at short DTE. → 02, 12
- **Dupire local volatility / Derman rule of two** — deterministic sigma_loc(K, T)^2 = [dC/dT + (r - q) K dC/dK + q C]/(1/2 K^2 d2C/dK2), or (dw/dT)/g(k) in total-variance form; fits the whole vanilla surface exactly but implies flattening forward smiles and rigid spot-vol co-movement (the "sticky local vol" regime).; rule of two: sigma_imp(K) ~ sigma_loc((F + K)/2), so local vol varies with level about twice as fast as implied vol with strike and short-dated implied skew ~ half the local-vol skew → 02, 03
- **Event variance** (vol clock; business-time clock) — total implied variance = diffusive variance on a business-time clock plus lumps at scheduled events: w(T) = sigma_d^2 tau(T) + sum v_e; extract from two expiries v_e ~ w(T2) - w(T1) - sigma_d^2(tau2 - tau1), or from two post-event expiries sigma_d^2 = (sigma2^2 T2 - sigma1^2 T1)/(T2 - T1) (the forward-variance formula) and v_e = (sigma1^2 - sigma_d^2) T1 (owner entry for this decomposition; Vol crush and Earnings strategies refer here); worked example 18% IV at 5 td without the event vs 30% at 10 td with it gives an implied 1-sd event move of 4.8%; the 7-day/35-day earnings example in 07 gives jump SD ~10.2%. → 02, 04, 07, 03
- **Forward price** — F = S e^{(r - q)T}, or S e^{rT} - FV(dividends) with discrete dividends; options are priced off F, not S; parity-implied F = K + e^{rT}(C - P); SPX uses "dividend points" (a discrete expected index-dividend stream per expiry) rather than a smooth q.; log-moneyness k = ln(K/F) is the natural x-axis of the smile → 02, 01, 12, 06
- **Forward variance** — sigma_fwd^2 = (sigma2^2 T2 - sigma1^2 T1)/(T2 - T1); under continuous paths the fair strike of a forward-starting variance swap; must be non-negative; example 20% at 3m and 22% at 6m gives 23.8% forward vol; forward variance claims starting more than ~1 month ahead earn roughly zero premium (Dew-Becker et al. 2017). → 02, 04, 08
- **Heston model** — dS = (r - q) S dt + sqrt(v) S dW1, dv = kappa(theta - v) dt + xi sqrt(v) dW2, corr rho; five parameters (v0, theta, kappa, xi, rho); SPX sanity ranges v0 0.02-0.05, theta 0.03-0.06, kappa 1-3, xi 0.4-1.0, rho -0.6 to -0.9 [experience, uncertain]; ATM skew decays ~1/T, too flat short-dated without jumps.; the Feller condition 2 kappa theta > xi^2 keeps variance positive and is routinely violated by SPX fits (Gatheral 2006 ratio ~0.62 [from memory]) → 02, 03
- **Hurst exponent** (H) — roughness parameter of the fractional Brownian motion driving log-vol; H ~ 0.1 from realized vol (Gatheral-Jaisson-Rosenbaum 2018), ~0.2 for equity indices in Mouti 2026 (cross-asset medians 0.05-0.20), ~0.3 option-implied; ATM skew term structure ~ T^{H - 1/2}. → 02
- **Implied volatility** (IV) — the sigma that makes BSM (or Black-76) reproduce the market price; a bijective quoting convention embedding the variance risk premium, skew effects and supply/demand, not a forecast; depends on a convention set (forward, rate, day-count, clock, model, mid vs last). → 02, 04, 12
- **IV solvers** — Newton-Raphson sigma_{n+1} = sigma_n - [BSM(sigma_n) - C_mkt]/vega (3 steps from a 0.30 start in the benchmark case) fails where vega ~ 0; bracketed Brent on [1e-4, 5] takes ~10-30 evaluations; Jäckel "Let's Be Rational" reaches machine precision in two iterations [not re-checked]; seed with Brenner-Subrahmanyam. → 02
- **Jump-diffusion** (Merton 1976; Kou) — diffusion plus Poisson jumps: Merton lognormal jumps (price = Poisson-weighted sum of BSM prices), Kou double-exponential; jumps alone give a negative short-dated skew that does not scale with sqrt(T); worked ATM call with lambda 0.1, mu_J -10%, delta 15% = 10.70 vs BSM 10.45.; Bates = Heston plus Merton jumps, the standard hybrid for short-dated (jump) and long-dated (SV) skew → 02
- **Lee moment formula** (2004) — asymptotic slope of total variance w(k) in |k| is at most 2, tied to the finite moments of S_T; IV grows at most like sqrt(2|k|/T); SVI bound b(1 + |rho|) <= 2; linear or flat IV wing extrapolation can violate it.; moment explosions (Andersen-Piterbarg 2007: E^Q[S_T^{1 + p}] infinite beyond a finite p* in Heston/SABR) make the bound bind, so far-OTM wings are mispriced by expansions such as Hagan's → 02
- **Model risk** — losses from using a model whose assumptions fail (fat tails, vol clustering, jumps, discrete hedging, costs, stochastic rates); every model calibrated to the vanilla surface prices path-independent European payoffs identically, so model choice matters only for path-dependent products and for hedges. → 02
- **No-arbitrage** (NA) — no self-financing strategy with zero cost, non-negative payoff and positive probability of gain; forces price = replication cost; parity/box/bound violations beyond costs in SPX/SPY are hit within milliseconds, leaving a band set by bid-ask, borrow and dividend uncertainty.; prices are discounted expectations under the risk-neutral measure Q (discounted traded prices are martingales), a pricing device that embeds risk premia and is not a forecast; N(d2) is the Q-probability of finishing ITM → 02, 03
- **Path-dependent volatility** (PDV; Guyon-Lekeufack 2023) — vol as a deterministic function of past returns; explains up to ~90% of equity-index IV variance and ~65% of future daily RV [not re-verified]; the live rival to rough volatility (2018-2026 debate). → 02
- **Put-call parity** — C - P = S e^{-qT} - K e^{-rT} = e^{-rT}(F - K) for Europeans (model-free); with discrete dividends C - P = S - PV(D) - K e^{-rT}; American bounds S - PV(D) - K <= C_A - P_A <= S - K e^{-rT}; regressing (C - P) on K gives the forward and discount factor in one step; borrow fees enter as PV(borrow). → 02, 01, 07, 12
- **Rough volatility** (RFSV; rough Bergomi; rough Heston) — log sigma_t = nu W^H_t with Hurst H ~ 0.1 (Gatheral-Jaisson-Rosenbaum 2018); rough Bergomi v_u = xi_0(u) exp(eta sqrt(2H) integral (u - s)^{H - 1/2} dW_s - ...); produces the power-law ATM skew term structure ~T^{-0.4} that Heston cannot. → 02
- **Rule of 16** — daily sigma ~ annual sigma/16 because sqrt(252) ~ 15.9; VIX 16 implies a ~1.0% daily 1-sigma, VIX 32 ~2.0%, VIX 15.27 ~0.96% (2026-10-13); realized SPX vol has run nearer VIX/22 (RV/VIX median 0.72, 2016-2026). → 02, 04, 11, 03
- **SABR** (Hagan et al. 2002) — dF = alpha F^beta dW1, d alpha = nu alpha dW2, corr rho; Hagan's lognormal-IV expansion in 02; rates default beta 0.5, equity slices beta 1 with rho -0.5 to -0.8, nu 0.5-1.5 [experience]; beta and rho nearly collinear; expansion gives negative density below ~F e^{-2 sigma sqrt(T)} for long T or large nu sqrt(T); Bartlett delta adds a Vega rho nu term. → 02, 03
- **Skew-stickiness ratio** (SSR; Bergomi 2009) — SSR = (d sigma_ATM/d ln S)/(d sigma/dk at ATM): sticky delta 0, sticky strike 1, local vol -> 2 at short maturity; SPX measured ~1.5 from one month to a year [from memory; verify], reportedly rising toward 2 in sell-offs [uncertain]; smile-adjusted delta = Delta_BSM + (vega/S)(SSR - 1) d sigma/dk. → 02, 04, 03
- **Smile / skew / smirk** — IV as a function of strike at fixed expiry: smile = both wings above ATM; skew/smirk = asymmetric, for equity indices the put wing is steep and IV rises as K falls; the call wing turned up in the 2020-21 and 2024-25 upside-call demand regimes [uncertain]; sources: fat tails and jumps, leverage/feedback, post-1987 crash-protection demand, correlation spikes, single-name events. → 02, 04
- **Static arbitrage / total implied variance** — total implied variance w(k, T) = sigma_imp(k, T)^2 T is the object on which arbitrage conditions, SVI/SSVI and the event-variance decomposition are stated; static arbitrage = arbitrage detectable from one price snapshot: butterfly (call convexity in K violated, equivalently negative RND; Durrleman condition g(k) >= 0 plus d_+ -> -inf), calendar (w decreasing in T at fixed k) and wing (Lee bound violated). → 02, 04
- **Sticky strike / sticky delta / sticky local vol** (Derman 1999 regimes) — sticky strike: sigma_imp(K) fixed when S moves, BSM delta correct, SSR = 1; sticky delta/moneyness: sigma_imp(k) fixed, smile rides with spot, SSR = 0; sticky local vol (implied tree): fixed-strike vols move with spot and ATM vol moves twice as fast as the strike skew, SSR -> 2; index markets empirically sit between strike and local-vol, so BSM over-hedges calls and under-hedges puts by ~6-11 delta points (1m ATM). → 02, 03, 04
- **Straddle-implied move** — ATM straddle ~ 0.8 S sigma sqrt(T) ~ risk-neutral expected absolute move (0.8 of the 1-sigma expected move), so 1-sigma ~ 1.25 x straddle (broker convention 0.85 x straddle); implied sd ~ straddle/(0.8 S); the straddle is ~0.8 SD, not 1 SD (common double-count error). → 02, 04, 07, 13
- **SVI / SSVI** (Gatheral; Gatheral-Jacquier 2014) — raw SVI slice w(k) = a + b[rho(k - m) + sqrt((k - m)^2 + sigma^2)] with a + b sigma sqrt(1 - rho^2) >= 0 and b(1 + |rho|) <= 2; SSVI surface w(k, theta) = (theta/2)[1 + rho phi k + sqrt((phi k + rho)^2 + 1 - rho^2)] with no-static-arbitrage conditions (dtheta/dT >= 0; theta phi(1 + |rho|) < 4; theta phi^2(1 + |rho|) <= 4); power-law phi gives ATM skew rho eta/(2 sqrt(T)), decaying like 1/sqrt(T). → 02
- **Variance swap** — forward contract paying N_var x (realized variance - K_var^2) with RV = (252/N) sum ln(S_i/S_{i-1})^2 x 100^2 (close-to-close, no mean subtraction); fair strike (Demeterfi-Derman-Kamal-Zou 1999) = (2 e^{rT}/T)[integral P(K)/K^2 dK below F + integral C(K)/K^2 dK above F]; K_var ~ sigma_ATM sqrt(1 + 3 T b^2) for linear skew b; vega notional: see Vega notional / variance notional; down-jumps make RV exceed the log-contract value (Carr-Wu 2009); single-name swaps commonly capped at 2.5x strike after 2008.; the log contract ln(S_T/F) (f'' = -1/K^2) has Q-expectation -1/2 E[integral sigma^2 dt] under continuous paths, which is why the 1/K^2 strip prices variance without a vol model (the VIX construction) → 02, 04, 07, 03, 08

## 3. Greeks and hedging

- **Bartlett delta** — SABR delta with an added term proportional to Vega x rho x nu that lets alpha co-move with F; the SABR analogue of the minimum-variance delta [from training]. → 03, 02
- **Beta-weighted delta** — sum_i Delta_i x S_i x beta_i / S_index (dollar form multiplies by 100 n_i); first-order cross-name netting that ignores idiosyncratic and correlation risk. → 03, 07, 13
- **Book break-even vol** — sigma_be = sqrt(-2 Theta_book/(Gamma_book S^2)) (r = q = 0, else theta net of carry): the dollar-gamma-weighted average IV of a book; a long-gamma book gains next day if |move| > S sigma_be/16. → 03
- **Break-even move** (IV/16 rule) — daily move at which gamma P&L equals theta loss: |dS/S| = sigma_i sqrt(dt) per day for ATM (see Rule of 16); 20% IV => 1.25-1.26% per trading day; worked 30-day example: gamma P&L for 1.26% = $5.50 vs theta/day $6.31, the $0.81 gap being rate carry. → 03, 02, 13
- **Charm** (delta bleed) — dDelta/dt = dTheta/dS; delta decay as calendar time passes with spot unchanged; large for OTM/ITM near expiry, near-the-money |charm| ~ 1/T; 25-delta put with 30 days: ~0.3 delta points/day, with 2 days: ~8 points/day; charm_put = charm_call - q e^{-qT}. → 03, 05
- **Daily P&L explain** — Taylor attribution dP&L ~ Delta dS + 1/2 Gamma dS^2 + Theta dt + Vega dsigma + Vanna dS dsigma + 1/2 Volga dsigma^2 + Rho dr + carry + unexplained, with Greeks at the prior close and sticky-strike dsigma; worked line 0.778 explained vs 0.772 full revaluation; a persistent residual signals a stale vol mark, wrong dividend/rate or hedging noise. → 03, 07, 13
- **Delta** — dV/dS, the hedge ratio in units of underlying per option; BSM call delta e^{-qT} N(d1) in (0, e^{-qT}), put -e^{-qT} N(-d1); not the probability of finishing ITM (that is N(d2); the gap ~0.4 sigma sqrt(T) ATM, ratio delta/N(d2) 1.14 ATM to 1.38 at K = 130 for 1y 20%-vol calls). → 03, 02, 05, 13
- **Delta hedging** — holding -Delta units of underlying against a long option (+Delta against a short) so first-order spot risk is zero; SPX dealers hedge in ES, single-stock dealers in the stock; futures hedge ratio Delta_F = Delta_S e^{-(r - q) T_F}. → 03, 05
- **Delta-hedged P&L identity** (fundamental theorem of derivatives trading) — dP&L = 1/2 Gamma S^2 (sigma_r^2 - sigma_i^2) dt: a hedged long option earns when realized exceeds implied variance, weighted by dollar gamma along the path (largest near K), so total P&L is path-dependent; holding options in 1/K^2 proportion removes the path dependence. → 03, 02, 05, 07
- **Discrete-hedging error** — random P&L from rebalancing at finite intervals; mean ~0, std ~ c sigma Vega/sqrt(N) with c = 1/sqrt(2) = 0.71 (constant gamma) or sqrt(pi/4) = 0.89 (Kamal 1998); std/premium ~ sqrt(pi/(4N)): daily hedging of a 1-month ATM (N ~ 21) leaves ~16-19% of premium, weekly ~44%, N > ~300 for 5%. → 03, 02
- **Dollar Greeks** — Greeks scaled to P&L units per contract or book: delta dollars = Delta S m; gamma per 1% = Gamma S (0.01 S) m; gamma P&L for x% = 0.5 Gamma (0.01 x S)^2 m; vega per vol point = Vega/100 x m; theta per day = Theta/365 x m (m = 100); common unit errors: vega per 1.00 vs per point (100x), gamma per $1 vs per 1% (S/100), calendar vs trading-day theta (365/252 = 1.45x). → 03, 13
- **Epsilon / psi** — dV/dq, dividend-yield sensitivity; negative for calls, positive for puts; 1y ATM call loses ~0.62 per 1% rise in yield; dividend misstatement shifts every Greek through the forward. → 03
- **Gamma** — d2V/dS2 = dDelta/dS, same for call and put, >= 0 for vanilla longs; BSM Gamma = e^{-qT} phi(d1)/(S sigma sqrt(T)); ATM gamma ~ 0.4/(S sigma sqrt(T)) diverges as 1/sqrt(T) into expiry (one hour to expiry at S = 100, 20% IV: ~1.9 per share; a 0DTE ATM has ~4-5x a 30-day option's gamma at the open and ~10-12x with an hour left); higher IV lowers ATM gamma and spreads it over strikes. → 03, 05, 13
- **Gamma scalping** — re-hedging a long-gamma position after moves to lock in realized variance; expected profit = (realized - implied variance) weighted by dollar gamma; compare IV to realized vol sampled at the hedging frequency. → 03
- **Jump / gap risk** — hedged P&L across a discontinuous move V(S(1 + J)) - V(S) - Delta S J ~ 1/2 Gamma S^2 J^2 + (1/6) Speed S^3 J^3, realized at once with no theta accrual and not reduced by rehedging; the second-order term overstates for |J| > ~1 strike width (30-day ATM, 10% gap: 2.64 exact vs 3.47 Taylor); a 5% gap costs ~12 days of theta on a short 30-day ATM. → 03, 13
- **Leland adjustment** (1985) — transaction-cost-adjusted hedging vol sigma_L^2 = sigma^2 [1 + sqrt(2/pi) k/(sigma sqrt(dt))] for a short option, minus sign for a long one (k = round-trip proportional cost, dt = rehedge interval); valid for single-sign gamma only. → 03, 02
- **Minimum-variance delta** (MV delta; skew-adjusted / shadow delta) — Delta_MV = Delta_BS + Vega_BS x dE[sigma_imp]/dS; Hull-White (2017) estimate it empirically with a quadratic in delta; with spot-vol beta -0.5 to -1.0 vol points per +1% (1m ATM, calm) the 30-day ATM call delta falls from 0.534 to ~0.42-0.48; cuts hedging-error variance for index options by a fifth to a quarter [uncertain]. → 03, 02
- **N(d2)** — risk-neutral probability a call finishes ITM; N(d1) = call delta is the probability under the share numeraire; "a 16-delta LEAP has a 16% chance ITM" overstates the risk-neutral probability by ~40%. → 03, 02, 13
- **Rho** — dV/dr; Rho_call = K T e^{-rT} N(d2) per 1.00 of r, positive for calls and negative for puts on stock; 1y ATM ~0.52 per 1% of rates vs 0.042 at 30 days; Black-76 rho = -T V for stock-style premium and 0 for futures-style premium. → 03
- **Speed / zomma / ultima / veta / color** — third-order Greeks: speed = dGamma/dS = -(Gamma/S)(d1/(sigma sqrt(T)) + 1); zomma = dGamma/dsigma = Gamma (d1 d2 - 1)/sigma; ultima = dVolga/dsigma; with gamma, charm and color they are O(1/sqrt(T)) or worse at the strike, so near expiry (and all day for 0DTE) analyze by full revaluation on a spot x vol grid (e.g. spot +/-3% in 0.25% steps x vol +/-10 points).; veta = dVega/dtau (+68.8/yr in the worked example), color = dGamma/dtau (-0.425/yr), with dVega/dt = -veta and dGamma/dt = -color → 03
- **Super-replication / uncertain volatility** — a BSM delta hedge run at a vol dominating true vol along every path yields terminal value >= payoff for convex payoffs (El Karoui, Jeanblanc-Picque, Shreve 1998); the Avellaneda-Levy-Paras (1995) uncertain-volatility model generalizes to a worst-case vol band; fails where gamma changes sign. → 03
- **Theta** — dV/dt, quoted per day; the rent paid for gamma via the BSM PDE Theta + 1/2 sigma^2 S^2 Gamma + (r - q) S Delta = r V (with r = q = 0, Theta = -1/2 sigma^2 S^2 Gamma); negative for long vanillas except deep-ITM European puts when r > 0 (S = 100, K = 150, 1y: +5.9/yr); ATM decay per day ~ value/(2 x days remaining), accelerating ~1/sqrt(T), while OTM/ITM theta peaks at 2-3 months and is nearly flat in the last two weeks; dividing calendar-time theta by 252 overstates decay by 45%. → 03, 01, 07
- **Vanna** — d2V/dS dsigma = dDelta/dsigma = dVega/dS = -e^{-qT} phi(d1) d2/sigma (call = put); zero at d2 = 0 (ATM-forward), positive for high strikes, negative for low strikes (30-day: -0.585 at K = 90, +0.941 at K = 110); peaks around 15-35-delta options with 1-3 months to expiry; a 25-delta 30-day put has vanna ~ -0.0134 per vol point. → 03, 05
- **Variance time / weekend vol crush** (Friday IV artifact) — dealers decay options in variance time (see Variance-time clock; heavier weight at open/close and scheduled prints, non-trading days weighted 0 to ~0.3 of a trading day [uncertain]), so calendar-time IV appears to fall Thursday-Friday and rebound Monday: artifact ~0.5-0.9 pt at 30 days, 2-4 pts at one week, 6-10 pts for an option expiring Monday-Tuesday; "weekend theta" on screens is mostly already in the price while weekend gap risk is not. → 03, 04, 07, 12
- **Vega** (kappa) — dV/dsigma, quoted per 1 vol point (= 0.01); Vega = S e^{-qT} phi(d1) sqrt(T), same for call and put; scales with sqrt(T) and peaks ATM (per vol point: 0.055 at 7d, 0.114 at 30d, 0.196 at 90d, 0.381 at 1y for S = 100, 20% vol); within one maturity Vega = Gamma S^2 sigma T, so a single-expiry book cannot be gamma-neutral without being vega-neutral; a vega bump must be specified as sticky-strike or sticky-delta. → 03, 02
- **Volga** (vomma) — d2V/dsigma2 = dVega/dsigma = Vega d1 d2/sigma; vol convexity; near zero ATM, peaks ~1.4 sd from the forward (|d1| ~ sqrt(2), around 8-10-delta strikes; K ~ 109 for 30 days at 20% vol, ~134 for 1y); when VVIX is high, vega P&L is convex and short-vega limits should shrink. → 03, 13
- **Weighted vega** (vega buckets; vol beta; tenor-weighted vega) — vega aggregated by maturity bucket and scaled by each tenor's IV sensitivity to a reference tenor (heuristic sqrt(T_ref/T), T_ref often 3m or 30 days; or mean-reverting beta (1 - e^{-kappa T})/(kappa T), 0.92 at 1m, 0.43 at 1y for kappa = 2); front-month IV moves ~1.7x the 3-month, so a raw-vega-neutral long calendar is net short weighted vega; fails when the long-run vol level shifts. → 03, 07, 13
- **Whalley-Wilmott band** (1997) — utility-optimal no-trade band around BSM delta with half-width (3/2 e^{-r(T - t)} k S Gamma^2/lambda)^{1/3}; widens as k^{1/3} with cost and |Gamma|^{2/3} with gamma; the practical replacement for "hedge on a clock". → 03, 07

## 4. Volatility and vol products

- **BXM** (Cboe S&P 500 BuyWrite Index) — long SPX plus a short nearest-term call at the listed strike closest to and above the pre-10 am index level, rolled on the third Friday at a 10:30-11:00 VWAP; launched 2002 with history from 1986-06-30; siblings BXMD (30-delta call), BXY (2% OTM), CMBO (2% OTM call plus ATM put); equity-like returns with ~25-35% lower sd since 1986 but drawdowns comparable to the index in 2008 and March 2020 [uncertain figures]. → 04, 07, 08, 13
- **Cboe benchmark indices** (PPUT, CLL1M/CLL3M, CNDR, BFLY, RXM, VXTH, PUTY, WPUT) — PPUT = SPX plus a rolling monthly 5%-OTM put (2022: -19.8% vs SPX -19.4%); CLL3M = 95/110 collar rolled quarterly, CLL1M = 95/100 monthly (Cboe roll page read 2026-10-14); CNDR = short ~20-delta put and call with ~5-delta wings [uncertain]; BFLY = short ATM straddle with 5%-OTM wings [uncertain]; RXM = risk reversal; VXTH = SPX plus 30-delta 1-month VIX calls sized by the VIX futures level [uncertain]; PUTY = 2%-OTM put-write; WPUT = weekly put-write (history from 2006). → 07, 04, 10
- **Close-to-close estimator** — sigma^2 = A/(n - 1) sum (r_t - rbar)^2 with r_t = ln(C_t/C_{t-1}), A = 252 (365 for calendar/crypto); zero-mean variant A/n sum r_t^2; the 21-day version annualized by sqrt(252) is the RV used in 10. → 04, 10
- **Contango / backwardation** — contango = longer maturities above shorter, the calm norm (VIX3M > VIX on 92% of days 2009-2026, mean spread +1.9 points, 100% of days with VIX < 15); for VIX futures it prices mean reversion, not free carry; backwardation (inversion) = front above back (VIX > VIX3M, or cash VIX above the front future), the stress signature: 7.6% of sessions since 2010, almost always when VIX > 40 (contango share 0% in that bucket), almost never below 20. → 04, 10, 11
- **Convexity adjustment** — the gap by which a vol swap or ATM straddle is worth less than the square root of the variance-swap rate (Jensen: sqrt is concave); grows with vol of vol; ~1-2 vol points at 1-3 months for equity indices [uncertain]; also why a VIX future sits below sqrt(forward variance). → 04, 02
- **DSPX** (Cboe S&P 500 Dispersion Index) — index of the implied dispersion of S&P 500 constituents relative to the index; launched September 2023 (Sept 27 per article URL [uncertain]); the market gauge for the correlation premium (for the related COR1M/COR3M indices see Implied correlation, 04). → 05, 07, 11
- **Expected move** (EM) — 1-sigma price change implied by IV: EM = S x IV x sqrt(T) with T = DTE/365 on a 365-day IV; risk-neutral expected absolute move = 0.798 EM (see Straddle-implied move); S = 500, IV 20%, 30 DTE: EM = 28.7, straddle ~22.9; only 17.9% of SPX days exceeded the prior-day VIX-implied 1-sigma (2016-2026) vs 31.7% under a normal. → 04, 03, 07
- **GARCH family** (GARCH(1,1), GJR-GARCH, EGARCH) — GARCH(1,1): sigma_t^2 = w + a e_{t-1}^2 + b sigma_{t-1}^2, persistence a + b 0.95-0.99 for daily equity indices, half-life ln 0.5/ln(a + b); GJR adds a leverage term g 1{e < 0}; EGARCH models log variance; HAR-RV usually beats them in practice. → 04
- **HAR-RV** (Corsi 2009) — RV_{t+1} = b0 + b_d RV_t + b_w mean(RV_{t-4..t}) + b_m mean(RV_{t-21..t}) + e, often in logs, coefficients each ~0.2-0.4 [uncertain]; HAR-RV-IV adds ln VIX_t; the practical standard for realized-vol forecasting. → 04
- **Implied correlation** (COR1M / COR3M) — average pairwise correlation reconciling SPX IV with constituent IVs (successors of ICJ/JCJ): rho_imp ~ (sigma_I^2 - sum w_i^2 sigma_i^2)/((sum w_i sigma_i)^2 - sum w_i^2 sigma_i^2); exceeds realized on average and spikes to 0.8-0.9+ in liquidity shocks (2008, March 2020 [uncertain]); single-digit COR1M in mid-2024 [uncertain]. → 04, 07, 10, 11
- **IV rank / IV percentile** — IV rank = (IV - min52w)/(max52w - min52w) x 100; IV percentile = share of lookback days with IV below today's; rank is distorted for a year after a spike, percentile is blind to regime shifts; neither says vol is cheap (IVR 50 can mean IV 12 vs realized 8 after a calm; IVR 20 can mean IV 25 vs realized 35 after a crash). → 04, 07
- **Leverage effect** — negative correlation between returns and vol changes (Black 1976): SPX daily correlation with VIX changes about -0.8 (dVIX ~ -1.37 points per 1% SPX log return, 2016-2026); for indices feedback and demand channels appear to dominate the balance-sheet explanation [uncertain]. → 04, 13
- **Microstructure noise / volatility signature plot** — bid-ask bounce, discreteness and stale quotes dominate realized variance at fine sampling; the signature plot (RV vs sampling frequency) shows where; 5-minute returns (78 per session) are rarely beaten across ~400 estimators (Liu-Patton-Sheppard 2015). → 04, 06, 12
- **MOVE** — ICE BofA index of 1-month OTC Treasury-option implied yield vol in bp/yr (weights 2y 20% / 5y 20% / 10y 40% / 30y 20% [from memory]); MOVE/sqrt(252) = daily 1-sigma yield move (100 => ~6.3 bp); ~50-100 in the 2010s, ~160-165 March 2020 and Oct 2022, ~198-200 March 2023 [uncertain]. → 04, 10
- **OVX / GVZ / VXN / RVX / VSTOXX / DVOL** — Cboe crude-oil vol (via USO options; record close 325.15 on 2020-04-21, 2026 high 120.91 on 03-11), gold vol (via GLD), Nasdaq-100 vol (VXN/VIX median ratio 1.17, record 80.08 on 2020-03-16), Russell 2000 vol (RVX/VIX median 1.29, record 83.19), EURO STOXX 50 vol.; DVOL = Deribit BTC/ETH 30-day implied vol index (BTC typically 40-80 [uncertain]) → 04
- **PUT** (Cboe S&P 500 PutWrite Index) — short nearest-term ATM SPX put collateralized in 1- and 3-month T-bills, rolled on the third Friday; launched 2007 with history from 1986; ~PUT ~ BXM by parity with slightly higher return and lower vol from roll timing and skew [uncertain]; 1986-2018 about 9-10% annualized at ~10% vol vs 15% for SPX, 2008 drawdown ~-33% vs ~-51% [Cboe-commissioned, uncertain]; monthly PUT beat weekly WPUT 2006-2015 (Bondarenko 2016). → 04, 07, 13, 08
- **Range estimators** — Parkinson (1980) sigma^2 = A/(4 n ln 2) sum [ln(H/L)]^2 (~5x efficiency vs close-to-close); Garman-Klass (1980) adds ln(C/O) (~7.4x); Rogers-Satchell (1991) drift-independent; Yang-Zhang (2000) combines overnight, open-close and RS variances with k = 0.34/(1.34 + (n + 1)/(n - 1)). → 04
- **Realized variance, intraday** (RV) — sum of squared 5-minute returns (M = 78 per 6.5-hour session) plus the overnight squared return; sigma_ann = sqrt(252 x mean RV_day); use noise-robust estimators (realized kernel, pre-averaging, two-scale) below ~1-minute sampling. → 04, 12
- **Realized volatility** (RV) — vol computed from observed prices over a past window; estimator-dependent; SPX 21-day RV median 12.5%, mean 15.0%, p90 24.7%, max 95.6% (window from 2020-03-06) over 2016-2026; RV exceeded VIX on 83 of 251 sessions in 2022 and 65 in 2018. → 04, 10
- **Roll yield** — convergence of each VIX future toward spot over a holding period, not a cash flow from rolling; long P&L ~ (change in spot) - (slope the curve priced in); a front-second spread of s%/month bleeds ~s%/month if spot is unchanged (typical contango bleed 5-10%/month [uncertain]); daily-rebalanced products add variance drag L(L - 1) sigma^2/2 per year (~49%/yr at 70% futures vol for L = -1 or 2; the close-rebalance flow itself is under LETF rebalancing). → 04
- **SKEW index** — Cboe index = 100 - 10 x risk-neutral 30-day skewness of SPX log returns from the VIX strip; 100 = lognormal; median 129.2 since 2010; record 183.12 on 2025-02-18 one session before the SPX peak, low 110.34 on 2022-11-03; Gram-Charlier table: SKEW 120 => P(2-sd down) 7.70%; a positioning gauge, steepest in calm markets, not a timing signal. → 04, 10
- **Stylized facts (clustering, persistence, long memory)** (stylized facts) — large absolute returns follow large absolute returns (SPX |r| autocorrelation 0.37 at lag 1, 0.14 at lag 21, 2016-2026); vol shocks decay slowly (log-VIX daily AR(1) 0.98, half-life ~34 trading days); |r| autocorrelations decay hyperbolically (Ding-Granger-Engle 1993); plus mean reversion, fat tails and the leverage effect. → 04
- **Term structure** — ATM IV or VIX-family level versus maturity; medians through 2026-10-13: VIX1D 14.0 (from 2022-05), VIX9D 15.2 (2011-), VIX 16.7 (2010-; 17.6 over 1990-2026), VIX3M 19.2 (2009-), VIX6M 21.5 (2008-), VIX1Y 22.6 (2007-); skew per unit log-moneyness flattens ~1/sqrt(T), so 25-delta risk reversals must be normalized across expiries; the ATM skew term structure decays ~T^{-0.4} for SPX. → 04, 02, 10
- **Variance risk premium** (VRP; volatility risk premium) — implied variance minus subsequently realized variance (loosely VIX minus subsequent 21-day RV in vol points); positive on average because dealers warehouse net put demand, jump risk is priced and variance is a priced state variable; owner-file estimate (04, 2016-10 to 2026-10): mean +3.7, median +4.7 vol points, positive on 84% of days, worst -72 (window from 2020-02-20), by VIX bucket +2.3 (<15) to +6.8 (>30); academic samples: Carr-Wu 1996-2003 log premium -0.59/month (~4.7 vol points per $1 vega), BTZ 1990-2007 VIX ~20.0 vs realized ~13.4; concentrated in the front month and the put wing; ~2,500 overlapping days are only ~120 independent windows. Sign convention differs by paper (Carr-Wu report RV - SW). → 04, 08, 02, 03, 07, 13
- **Vega notional / variance notional** — N_vega = 2 K_var N_var (dollar P&L per vol point for small moves); variance-swap P&L = N_var (RV - K^2); example N_vega $100k, K 20: N_var 2,500, realized 25 pays $562,500 (vs $500,000 if linear); with K 20 and expected RV 16% (expected carry 0.0144 per $1 variance notional), realized 80 loses 0.60, ~42x the expected carry (08 worked example). → 04, 02, 07, 08, 10
- **VIX** (Cboe Volatility Index) — model-free 30-day expected SPX variance from a 1/K^2-weighted strip of OTM SPX/SPXW mid-quotes in the two expiries bracketing 30 days (23-37 DTE, Friday expiries, since 2014-10-06), square-rooted and x100; sigma_T^2 = (2/T) sum (dK_i/K_i^2) e^{RT} Q(K_i) - (1/T)(F/K0 - 1)^2 with T in minutes/525,600; strike selection stops after two consecutive zero-bid-or-ask strikes (since 2025-02-10); disseminated every 15 s; history from Jan 1990 (back-filled), current methodology since 2003-09-22; 1990-2026: mean 19.4, median 17.6, p90 28.5, record close 82.69 (2020-03-16), intraday 89.53 (2008-10-24), low close 9.14 (2017-11-03). A price (variance-swap rate), not an average IV and not a crash forecast.; VXO = the former S&P 100 (OEX) index it replaced in 2003, back-calculated ~150 on 1987-10-19 [uncertain] → 04, 02, 10, 11
- **VIX ETPs** (VXX, VIXY, UVXY, UVIX, SVXY, SVIX, XIV, TVIX) — exchange-traded products on the S&P 500 VIX Short-Term Futures (30-day constant-maturity) index: VXX/VIXY 1x long, UVXY 1.5x (2x before late Feb 2018 [uncertain]), UVIX 2x (launched 2022 with SVIX [uncertain]), SVXY -0.5x (-1x before Feb 2018), SVIX -1x (inception 2022-03-28, index SHORTVOL), XIV -1x (terminated after 2018-02-05), TVIX delisted 2020 [uncertain]; leveraged/inverse products rebalance at the 4:15 pm settlement (flow formula: see LETF rebalancing); a 1x long decays ~40-70% in a calm year [uncertain]; VXX creations were suspended March 2022 after a Barclays over-issuance error [uncertain]. → 04, 05, 10
- **VIX futures** (VX) — forward on VIX at expiry, i.e. on the square root of the 30-day variance-swap rate starting then; $1,000 multiplier, cash-settled to VRO; launched March 2004 (March 24 per Cboe PDF, 26 per CFE/Wikipedia); nine monthly and six weekly listings (weeklys since 2015; as of 2026-10-14); front-month beta to spot ~0.5-0.7 (higher in backwardation), constant-maturity products capture about half a spot move [practitioner estimate]; the futures premium falls, often below zero, as risk rises (Cheng 2019). → 04, 07, 08, 10
- **VIX options** — European, cash-settled Wednesday mornings to VRO, $100 multiplier, launched Feb 2006; priced off the matching VIX future with Black-76, never off spot (in backwardation deep-ITM calls trade below spot intrinsic); positively skewed smile (OTM call IV routinely >100% [uncertain]); VIX options ADV 862K in 2025; SEC approved "VIX Future-Option Orders" (packaged execution of VIX options with VX futures, Rel. 34-106216, SR-CBOE-2026-004) on 2026-09-02; options on VX futures themselves are a planned CFE product (OCC Aug-2026 notice), neither approved nor launched as of 2026-10-14. → 04, 01, 02, 07, 11
- **VIX1D / VIX9D / VIX3M / VIX6M / VIX1Y** — VIX-family siblings at 1 business day (VIX1D: built from today's PM-settled SPXW and the next expiry in business minutes, 405 per RTH session, near-term frozen ~60 minutes before 4:00 pm; launched 2023-04-24, history from 2022-05-13; median 14.0, min 7.01 on 2024-05-21, max close 81.89 on 2025-04-04), 9 days, 3 months (93 days; history from 2009-09-18; 17.96 on 2026-10-13), 6 months and 1 year. → 04, 10, 11
- **VIX/VIX3M ratio** — term-structure stress ratio: < 0.9 calm, 0.9-1.0 caution, > 1.0 inverted, > 1.2 crash regime; > 1.0 on 7.6% and > 1.2 on 0.5% of sessions since 2010; record 1.344 on 2020-02-28; longest run above 1: 43 sessions (2020-02-24 to 04-23); a regime marker, not a bottom signal. → 10, 04
- **Vol crush / ex-event vol / kink** — an event adds jump variance to the front expiry (a kink in annualized IV); after the event it is removed and front IV collapses even after a large move; ex-event (diffusive) vol and event move from two expiries (decomposition: see Event variance; 7-day 60% and 35-day 35% IVs give diffusive 25.1% and a 7.5% 1-sd move); the post-event "vanna drift" trade is practitioner folklore without peer-reviewed support.; mechanical event drop = the fall in the 30-day VIX when a scheduled event's excess variance leaves the window with diffusive vol unchanged, VIX_after ~ sqrt(VIX_before^2 - E x (365/30) x 10^4) (~0.5 points for a priced 1.5% FOMC move at VIX 15); VIX1D shows the event premium directly → 04, 02, 07, 05, 08
- **Vol of vol / VVIX** — variability of IV; VVIX = VIX-method 30-day implied vol of VIX options (history from 2006-03-06), dominated by the call wing; 2008-2026 median 91.4, p90 115.7; records 207.59 (2020-03-16), 180.61 (2018-02-08), 177.34 (2018-02-05), 173.32 (2024-08-05), 170.92 (2025-04-08); dollar price of VIX convexity scales with VIX x VVIX; folklore thresholds ~85 cheap / ~120 rich. → 04, 10, 13
- **Vol targeting** (vol control; risk parity; CTAs) — exposure scaled to target sigma / trailing realized sigma with 1-3 month lookbacks and a cap (w_t = min(w_max, sigma_target/sigma_hat)); because RV is trailing, selling persists for 1-10 days after a shock and re-leveraging follows calm weeks; suppresses RV and the VRP in calm regimes; aggregate equity exposure estimates range $300bn to >$1tn [contested]. → 04, 05, 10, 13
- **Volatility vs variance** — vol = annualized standard deviation of log returns in percent, variance = vol^2; option prices are roughly linear in vol near the money while variance-swap payoffs are linear in variance; annualizing with sqrt(365) instead of sqrt(252) misstates vol by ~20%. → 04, 12

## 5. Dealer flows and positioning

- **Air pocket** — abrupt dealer underlying selling and vol buying at or below an autocallable barrier: above the barrier dealers are long the down-and-in put's vega and |delta| and add long-delta hedges into the decline; at knock-in the DIP becomes a vanilla put with lower vega/|delta|, so hedges are dumped at once (HSCEI 2015-16, Korea ELS 2024, Nikkei 2024-08-05). → 05, 10
- **Anti-pinning** — repulsion from a strike when dealers are net short gamma there; Golez-Jackwerth (2012) find S&P futures pulled toward the ATM strike at serial futures-option expiries but pushed away from the carry-adjusted ATM strike before AM-settled SPX expiries (>= $115m notional shift per expiration day). → 05, 03, 08
- **Autocallable** (structured note; down-and-in put, DIP) — retail note paying digital coupons while the investor is implicitly short a down-and-in put; the dealer is long the DIP and short the coupon strip, so dealers sell long-dated index vol in calm markets (Nikkei/KOSPI/HSCEI/SX5E, increasingly SPX) and can flip to buying vol as spot falls through barriers [mechanics uncertain]; US structured-note issuance >$195bn in 2025, autocalls >43% [SRP, not re-verified]. → 05, 10
- **Buffer / defined-outcome ETF** — fund holding FLEX options (long deep-ITM call, long ATM put, short put at the buffer, short call at the cap) reset on fixed annual/quarterly dates; $78bn across ~420 ETFs at end-2025 (Morningstar); the dealer side is long gamma at cap and buffer and short gamma near the reset-date ATM; FLEX positions sit outside standard chains and OI feeds. → 05, 01, 09
- **Call wall / put wall** — strikes with the largest modeled call gamma / put gamma; practitioner resistance/support proxies whose effect depends on who holds the position and on execution-window liquidity. → 05
- **Cboe Open-Close Volume Summary** (open-close data) — licensed per-exchange daily (and 1/10-minute intraday) volume split by capacity (customer, professional customer, broker-dealer, market maker) x buy/sell x open/close with customer size buckets (<100 / 100-199 / >199); C1 history to 2005; "customer" includes institutions and hedging funds, not only retail. → 05, 12
- **Charm flow** — predictable hedge adjustment from delta decay: with spot unchanged OTM |delta| decays toward 0 at rate ~1/T, so dealers short OTM puts buy back short-futures hedges, strongest at the expiration-day open and in the last hour (0DTE clustering in the last 30-60 minutes); reverses if dealer-short puts go ITM; formula charm flow = -sum N_i Charm_i (1/365) M S. → 05, 03
- **Covered-call ETFs / ELNs** (JEPI, JEPQ, XYLD, QYLD) — derivative-income funds selling ~1-month calls 2-4% OTM, JEPI/JEPQ via equity-linked notes whose issuing bank is long the embedded calls in laddered weekly tranches; whoever hedges them supplies long gamma that leans against rallies; JEPI ~$46bn (July 2026 [uncertain]), category >$100bn by 2025 [uncertain]; the largest arm of structural vol supply. → 05, 10, 13, 07
- **Dealer / option market maker** (OMM) — liquidity provider quoting two-sided markets for spread plus an inventory risk premium and delta-hedging in the underlying; "dealer" = aggregate OMM capacity; hedges delta near-continuously, cannot hedge gamma/vega with the underlying, so recycles inventory and runs dollar-gamma/vega/vanna limits; nets delta across SPX/SPXW/XSP/SPY/ES/NDX/QQQ/VIX/OTC before hedging in ES. → 05, 03
- **Dealer inventory** — approximately the negative of aggregate customer net positioning per series; an approximation, not an identity (OMM-to-OMM trades, crosses, other capacities, cross-product offsets); price impact comes from the net gamma of delta-hedgers, not of all holders. → 05
- **Dealer long gamma / short gamma** — long gamma: position delta rises with S, so the dealer sells rallies and buys dips (vol-dampening, intraday mean reversion, pinning); short gamma: delta falls as S rises, so the dealer buys rallies and sells dips (vol-amplifying, trend days, anti-pinning); end users are net long index puts and short upside calls, so dealers are typically short downside gamma and long upside gamma; Amaya et al. (2025) find SPX OMM gamma positive on average with a bounded worst-case impact of +3.3 daily vol points. → 05, 03, 08, 11, 13
- **Demand-based option pricing** (Garleanu-Pedersen-Poteshman 2009) — end-user net demand raises an option's price in proportion to the variance of its unhedgeable part; the mechanism behind negative delta-hedged index option returns and steep index skew. → 08, 05, 02, 03
- **Gamma exposure** (GEX; dollar gamma) — aggregate dealer gamma expressed as dollars of underlying to trade per 1% move: dealer $gamma per 1% = sum N_i Gamma_i M S^2 0.01; the naive SqueezeMetrics (2016/2017) estimate GEX_strike = Gamma x OI x M x S^2 x 0.01 x s assumes dealers long every call (s = +1) and short every put (s = -1), a modeling assumption that is exactly wrong for speculative single-name call buying; OI has no side, 0DTE OI is stale, and GEX is strongly negatively correlated with VIX. → 05, 03
- **Gamma flip / zero gamma** (Volatility Trigger) — modeled spot level where aggregate dealer gamma changes sign (long above, short below, typically as spot falls into the put strikes); SpotGamma's "Volatility Trigger" is a reportedly proprietary variant [uncertain]; precision is illusory given the GEX caveats. → 05, 03
- **Gamma squeeze** — customer call buying => dealer short calls => dealer buys stock => price rises => call deltas rise => more buying; needs dealer net short calls, thin liquidity and OI near spot; IV falling as price rises is the counter-signal; the SEC's GME staff report (2021-10-18) found growth mostly in puts and "not consistent with a gamma squeeze", a reading Mitts et al. (2022) dispute. → 05, 10, 13
- **HIRO** — SpotGamma's real-time hedging-impact estimate (vendor product); one of several vendor level sets (SpotGamma, SqueezeMetrics/Prior Analytics, Menthor Q, Tier1Alpha, GammaEdge, Nomura) that are not mutually comparable. → 05
- **Informational vs non-informational channel** — informed option trades delta-hedged at inception transmit information into the stock (Pan-Poteshman 2006; Hu 2014); later hedge rebalancing from gamma/charm/vanna moves prices and volatility independent of information (Ni-Pearson-Poteshman-White 2021). → 05, 08
- **JHEQX** (JPM Hedged Equity collar) — S&P 500 stock portfolio plus a quarterly SPX put-spread collar (long ~5% OTM put, short ~20% OTM put, short ~3-5% OTM call), rolled on the last trading day of each quarter; fund $18.52bn (2026-06-30); legs ~40-45k contracts [practitioner estimate]; dealers long gamma around the short call (a lid into expiry) and short gamma around the 5% put; roll-day net dealer buying ~0.5 x contracts x multiplier x S when the old call is deep ITM, often pre-hedged. → 05
- **LETF rebalancing** (leveraged/inverse ETF close rebalance) — an L x leveraged ETF trades (L^2 - L) x AUM x r_day at the close in the direction of the day's move (Cheng-Madhavan 2009): L = +2 or -1 => 2 A r, +3 or -2 => 6 A r, -3 => 12 A r; $20bn of 3x products on a -2% day => $2.4bn selling into the close; aggregate US leveraged equity ETF close flow ~$5-20bn per 1% [industry estimates]; single-stock LETFs (2022-) concentrate the flow in TSLA/NVDA-type names (~$10bn per 1% at the June 2026 peak [Nomura, uncertain]); predictable flows get front-run and partly reverse next day. → 05, 06, 04, 10
- **Max pain** — strike minimizing the total intrinsic value of expiring options; folk theory that price is pulled there; pinning is mostly hedge rebalancing, manipulation evidence is limited, and the one supportive study (0.4%/week in small illiquid stocks, authors/venue unverified) is unreplicated. → 05
- **MBS convexity hedging / NY cut / sovereign oil hedge** — cross-asset analogues of the same loop: MBS prepayment options leave holders short convexity, so duration hedging amplifies rate moves (1994, summer 2003 [attribution uncertain]); large FX vanilla expiries at the 10:00 NY cut pull spot toward big strikes; Mexico's sovereign oil put (~$1bn premium, payouts ~$5bn 2015 and ~$2.5bn 2020 [uncertain]) leaves dealers selling futures into declines. → 05
- **Pinning** (price pinning; strike clustering) — tendency of an optionable stock to close at or near a high-OI strike on expiration because net-long-gamma market makers sell above and buy below it (Avellaneda-Lipkin 2003 coupling beta = n E/(sqrt(2 pi) sigma^2 T)); Ni-Pearson-Poteshman (2005) measure a 16.5 bp average return shift (~$9bn market cap) per expiration, 1996-2002 [not re-verified]; cash-settled SPX does not pin like a stock; magnitude in fragmented, high-0DTE markets is uncertain. → 05, 01, 03, 06, 08
- **Portfolio insurance** — 1980s synthetic-put replication that sells futures as the market falls (hedge ratio h = -N(-d1)); short gamma without having bought the option; ~$60-90bn insured in Oct 1987 and up to 24% of futures short volume on 1987-10-19 [secondary]; the template for every forced-seller loop. → 05, 10
- **Post-OPEX hangover** — Monday unwind of delta inherited through PM/physically-settled exercise and assignment; partly inventory clean-up, not information; the "window of strength/weakness" OPEX narrative (vanna/charm buying into OPEX, loss of support after) is practitioner folklore without peer-reviewed support. → 05
- **Systematic option flows** — the calendar of rule-based option supply and demand: JHEQX quarter-end roll, covered-call ETF weekly tranches, buffer-ETF reset days, autocallable issuance and barriers, vol-targeting and CTA rebalancing, dispersion books, LETF closes, VIX ETP 4:15 pm rebalances, index reconstitutions (Russell semi-annual from 2026: effective 2026-06-26 and 2026-12-11), VIX settlement mornings; perfectly predictable flows decay as they are front-run. → 05, 06, 10
- **Vanna exposure / vanna flow** (VEX; vanna rally) — aggregate dealer dDelta/dsigma: when IV falls with spot unchanged, |delta| of dealer-short OTM puts shrinks and dealers buy back futures (vanna flow = -sum N_i Vanna_i 0.01 M S; 10,000 short 25-delta puts and a 2-point IV drop => ~$160m of futures buying at SPX 6000); when IV rises they sell more, so negative spot-vol correlation makes vanna a two-way feedback loop; a one-off delta shift, reversed if IV re-rises. → 05, 03

## 6. Microstructure and intraday

- **23x5 trading / Overnight Price Bands** — SEC-approved exchange overnight sessions (24X, Nasdaq, NYSE Arca, Cboe EDGX, MEMX) 9:00 pm-4:00 am ET Sunday through Thursday nights, limit orders only, after a mandatory 8-9 pm industry pause (NYSE Arca accepts orders from 8:59 pm; the 8 pm-4 am window is Blue Ocean ATS, not the exchanges), static +/-20% price bands from the adjusted official close and 7:45 pm last sale ($3 minimum, no automatic pauses), "T" sale condition prints, an official overnight NBBO and a CAT trade date starting at 8:00 pm; industry launch target 2026-12-06; options excluded; the overnight gap becomes a continuous path. → 06, 11, 09
- **ATS / dark pool / TRF** — FINRA-regulated off-exchange matching (mostly midpoint, no pre-trade display) whose trades print to a FINRA trade reporting facility; off-exchange share of US share volume crossed 50% around the turn of 2024/25 (record ~51.8% Jan 2025 [uncertain]); Blue Ocean ATS runs the 8 pm-4 am overnight session (~83% of overnight volume Dec 2025 [self-reported]). → 06
- **Authorized Participant / creation unit / iNAV** — AP creates or redeems ETF creation units (SPY: 50,000 shares) in kind against the index basket; iNAV/IOPV disseminated every 15 s; arbitrage keeps SPY within ~1-2 bps of iNAV in RTH; SPY NAV ~ index/10 plus accrued undistributed dividends, stepping down on SPY ex-dates. → 06
- **Basis / fair value** — futures fair value F = S exp((r - q)T) (desk approximation S(1 + (r - q)T)); basis = F - S, positive when r > q (S = 6000, r 4.0%, q 1.3%, 60 days: fair basis ~+26.7 pts); observed basis = fair basis + an implied-financing premium from dealer balance sheet and roll demand. → 06, 02
- **BTIC / TACO** — CME Basis Trade at Index Close (since 2015): a futures trade priced at the official S&P 500 cash close plus a basis negotiated intraday; Trade at Cash Open prices at the next cash open plus basis; transfers closing-benchmark demand from the equity auctions into futures, and the BTIC basis is a live read of close pressure. → 06
- **Call auction** (opening/closing; MOO/LOO; MOC/LOC; Closing D Order; CO/IO orders; NOII/EOII) — single-price auction at 9:30 and 4:00 crossing all eligible interest at the volume-maximizing price; MOO/LOO = market/limit-on-open, MOC/LOC = market/limit-on-close; NYSE: imbalance to Floor brokers from 2:00 pm, MOC/LOC/CO entry until 3:50, Regulatory Closing Imbalance published at 3:50 if >= 500 round lots (no cancels after, RM-22-12), Closing D Orders (Floor-broker discretionary, >46% of closing volume Aug 2024 [source needs re-identification]) and CO/Closing IO orders (execute only against the imbalance, accepted until 4:00); Nasdaq Closing Cross: Early Order Imbalance Indicator (EOII) from 3:50, Net Order Imbalance Indicator (NOII) with near/far prices from 3:55, MOC until 3:55, LOC until 3:58, IO until 4:00; NYSE Arca: Core Close freeze 3:59-4:00 (SPY's official close, condition M); early-close days shift everything 3 hours earlier (MOC cutoff 12:50, Arca freeze 12:59). → 06, 12
- **Closing-auction share / J-shaped volume / U-shaped volatility** — closing auction ~9-10% of consolidated US volume on a typical 2024 day, ~20% on rebalance/expiry days, 34% of notional on Russell day 2024-06-28; NYSE record 3.57bn shares / $230.5bn on 2026-03-20; intraday volume is J-shaped (first 30 min ~8-12%, last 30 min incl. auction ~25-35% [vendor TCA ranges]) while per-minute volatility is U-shaped (first 15-30 min highest, second peak into the close); the lunchtime lull ~11:30-13:30 ET has the thinnest depth. → 06, 12
- **ES / MES daily settlement** — ES = CME E-mini S&P 500 future, $50 x index, 0.25-pt tick; MES $5; Globex ~23 hours (Sun 6 pm-Fri 5 pm ET, 5-6 pm break, 4:15-4:30 pm halt [uncertain]); daily settlement = lead-month VWAP over the 30-second window 3:59:30-4:00:00 pm ET (14:59:30-15:00:00 CT), aligned with the cash close since trade date 2020-10-26 (CFTC filing rule092420cmedcm001); before that the window ended at 4:15 pm ET; ES leads the cash index by seconds and is the only liquid hedge during Curb and GTH; overnight dynamic limit +/-5% in March 2020, later +/-7% [uncertain]. → 06, 01, 09, 13
- **Intraday momentum / hedging-demand channel** — first 30-minute return (from prior close) predicts the last 30-minute return (Gao-Han-Li-Zhou 2018, SPY 1993-2013); Baltussen et al. (2021, 60+ futures 1974-2020) find the last-30-minute return predicted by the prior-close-to-3:30 return and reversing over days, attributed to short-gamma hedgers, leveraged ETFs and late-informed trading; practitioner replications report attenuation after publication [uncertain]. → 06, 05, 08
- **Key reference levels** (PDH/PDL/PDC, ONH/ONL, opening range, floor pivots, VWAP bands, Market Profile) — prior-day high/low/close; overnight high/low from ES or premarket (4:00 pm-9:30 am); high/low of the first n minutes (5, 15, 30); pivots P = (H + L + C)/3, R1 = 2P - L, S1 = 2P - H; VWAP = sum(p v)/sum(v) with bands at +/-k sigma_VWAP; Market Profile (Steidlmayer) TPO distribution with POC, ~70% value area and 9:30-10:30 initial balance; documented channels are order clustering just beyond round numbers (Osler), VWAP benchmarking and strike hedging; technical trading rules (Brock-Lakonishok-LeBaron 1992) fail data-snooping adjustment after 1986 (Sullivan-Timmermann-White 1999, 7,846 rules), while Donaldson-Kim (1993) report DJIA price barriers at 100-multiples beyond chance (not re-tested by STW); any level claim must pass the 12 machinery (placebo levels, climatology, DM test) and no SPY prior-session reaction statistics may be computed. → 06, 12, 08
- **Kyle lambda / Glosten-Milgrom / Roll** — Kyle (1985): dP = lambda Q with lambda = sigma_v/(2 sigma_u), price change per unit net order flow with a strategic insider; Glosten-Milgrom: ask = E[V | buy], bid = E[V | sell], spread ~ pi (V_high - V_low) with informed share pi; Roll (1984): spread s = 2 sqrt(-Cov(dp_t, dp_{t-1})), bid-ask bounce gives negative autocorrelation in transaction prices. → 06
- **Limit order book / NBBO / SIP / round lot** — LOB = exchange queue of resting limit orders matched by price then time; NBBO = best bid/offer across protected quotes consolidated by the SIPs (CTA Tape A NYSE-listed, Tape B Arca/other-listed incl. SPY, UTP Tape C Nasdaq-listed); round lot = unit for protected quotes, tiered by price since 2025-11-03 (100 shares <= $250, 40 to $1,000, 10 to $10,000, 1 above); odd lots print to the SIP since 2013-12-09 (condition I, not last-sale eligible; 64.4% of trades Feb 2025) and odd-lot quotes enter core SIP data from 2026-05-01 (first business day of May 2026; depth-of-book between the NBBO deferred to May 2028) [go-live not confirmed]. → 06, 12, 09
- **LULD** (Limit Up-Limit Down) — per-security price bands around the trailing 5-minute average price (Tier 1 > $3: 5%; $0.75-$3: 20%; Tier 2 > $3: 10%; doubled 3:35-4:00 pm) pausing continuous trading for 5 minutes (extendable) after a 15-second Limit State, reopened by auction; pilot approved 2012-05-31 after the Flash Crash, permanent 2019-04-11; 1,278 halts on 2015-08-24; 40 GME pauses in Jan 2021; an underlying's LULD pause halts all its options. → 06, 09, 10, 13
- **MWCB** (market-wide circuit breaker) — S&P 500 declines of 7% (Level 1) and 13% (Level 2) vs prior close halt all equities and options for 15 minutes if before 3:25 pm ET; 20% (Level 3) halts the rest of the day (resumption 4:00 am next day under 23x5); effective 2013-04-08; tripped four times, all Level 1 (2020-03-09, -12, -16, -18); the old DJIA-points design tripped once (1997-10-27); breakers stop the clock, not the move. → 06, 09, 10, 13
- **Order-flow imbalance** (OFI; Cont-Kukanov-Stoikov 2014) — net change in best-level bid minus ask depth over short windows; mid-price changes ~ beta x OFI with beta ~ c/(average touch depth), so impact per share is highest when the book is thin (open, news, overnight). → 06
- **Overnight drift / overnight-intraday split** — ES close-to-close return earned almost entirely 2:00-3:00 am ET at the European open (~1.48 bp/day, ~3.7% annualized, 1998-2020, Boyarchenko-Larsen-Whelan) as futures market makers unwind the US closing imbalance; SPY cumulative close-to-open return exceeds open-to-close by a large multiple since 1993 [uncertain]; the overnight gap carries ~20-40% of close-to-close variance [uncertain; period-dependent]; NightShares NSPY tested the overnight-only exposure and closed 2023-07-31 (-6.9% vs +22% [uncertain]); delta-hedged SPX option returns are ~-1%/day close-to-open and +0.3% open-to-close (Muravyev-Ni 2020). → 06, 03, 08, 12
- **PFOF / wholesaler / Rules 605 and 606** — retail brokers route marketable orders to wholesalers (Citadel Securities, Virtu, G1/Susquehanna, Two Sigma, Jane Street, Hudson River [list uncertain]) who fill off-exchange at or inside the NBBO and pay for the flow; legal for options, where internalization must go through exchange price-improvement auctions; Rule 606 discloses per-venue routing and payments (options separately since 2018), Rule 605 execution-quality reports cover NMS stocks only; three wholesalers receive ~90% of options PFOF (Bryzgalova-Pavlova-Sikorskaya 2023); Citadel Securities paid ~$732m for options flow in 9M 2024. → 06, 09, 08, 11
- **Pre-FOMC drift / scheduled release times** — average S&P 500 excess return of ~49 bp in the 24 hours before scheduled FOMC statements, 1994-2011, >80% of the equity premium (Lucca-Moench 2015); essentially disappeared after 2015 (Kurov-Wolfe-Gilbert 2021); release clock (ET): 8:30 CPI/PPI/NFP/GDP/claims, 10:00 ISM/confidence/JOLTS, 11:30 European close, 1:00 Treasury auctions, 2:00 FOMC statement and 2:30 press conference (since April 2011); announcement vol/volume spikes last 30-60 minutes. → 06, 08
- **Reg NMS** (Rules 610/611/612/603; 2024 amendments) — Rule 611 order protection bars trade-throughs of protected round-lot quotes (ISO = intermarket sweep order exception; SEC proposed rescinding 611 and 610(d) on 2026-06-17, outcome open); Rule 612 sets the $0.01 tick for stocks >= $1; the 2024-09-18 amendments add a $0.005 tick for tight-spread stocks (TWAQS <= $0.015), a 10-mil access-fee cap and price-tiered round lots; tick/fee compliance deferred to the first business day of Nov 2027; round-lot tiers live 2025-11-03. → 06, 11, 09
- **Reg SHO Rule 201** — short-sale circuit breaker: after a >= 10% decline from the prior close, short sales only above the national best bid for the rest of the day and the next; makes bids look stickier below -10% in single names and leveraged ETFs on crash days. → 06
- **Spread measures** (quoted, effective, realized; price impact) — quoted = ask - bid; effective = 2 d (P - M), what a marketable order pays; realized = 2 d (P - M_{t+5min}), what the liquidity provider keeps; price impact = effective - realized (adverse-selection loss), temporary vs permanent; SPY quotes one tick (~0.15 bp) for nearly all of RTH with $10-50m displayed per side [uncertain]; for options the conventional effective spread overstates the cost actually paid by ~25% on average and >60% for execution-timing traders (Muravyev-Pearson 2020). → 06, 08, 12
- **Square-root law** (price impact) — metaorder cost ~ Y sigma_daily sqrt(Q/V) with Y ~ 0.5-1.0 (Almgren et al. 2005; Toth et al. 2011), exponent estimates 0.4-0.7, permanent share roughly half to two-thirds [uncertain]; V is execution-window volume, not full-day notional: $2.5bn of 0DTE hedging is ~4-8 bp over a $400bn ES day but ~10-25 bp in the last 30 minutes. → 06, 05
- **Stale open** — the published SPX cash index at 9:30-9:35 mixes opened stocks with prior closes of unopened constituents (DMMs open names one by one, algorithmically if within 10% of the reference price), so it lags SPY/ES and biases gap statistics; option quotes before ~9:30:30-9:45 are indicative noise; the same staggered opens make SET/SOQ differ from any printed 9:30 level. → 06, 09, 12, 01
- **Stub quote** — market-maker placeholder quote (e.g. $0.01) that printed in the 2010 Flash Crash when liquidity providers withdrew; the extreme form of conditional liquidity, in which SPX option quotes widen 5-10x or vanish when RV jumps (2010, 2015, 2024). → 10, 06
- **Variance ratio / MDH / 30-minute periodicity** — VR(q) = Var(r_{t,q})/(q Var(r_t)) = 1 + 2 sum (1 - k/q) rho_k (Lo-MacKinlay 1988): index daily autocorrelation was positive 1960s-1990s and is ~0 or slightly negative since the 2000s [uncertain] as ETF/futures arbitrage removed stale prices; the mixture-of-distributions hypothesis (returns and volume share a latent information-arrival rate) explains the volume-volatility link; Heston-Korajczyk-Sadka (2010) find return continuation at exact 30-minute multiples. → 06
- **VPIN** (volume-synchronized probability of informed trading; Easley-Lopez de Prado-O'Hara 2012) — VPIN = sum |V_i^buy - V_i^sell|/(n V_bucket) over volume buckets with bulk-classified sides; claimed to rise before the Flash Crash, but Andersen-Bondarenko (2014) find no predictive content beyond volume and volatility. → 06
- **VWAP / TWAP / implementation shortfall** — VWAP = sum(p_i v_i)/sum(v_i), TWAP = mean of equal-time slices; implementation shortfall (Perold 1988) = all-in cost vs the arrival price; desks benchmarked to VWAP attract passive flow and VWAP algos scheduled to the time-of-day curve reinforce the J-shape; faster execution raises impact, slower raises timing risk (Almgren-Chriss frontier). → 06, 12
- **XNYS calendar / session arithmetic** — ISO 10383 MIC for NYSE used by exchange_calendars (regular open 13:30 UTC in EDT, 14:30 in EST; early close 13:00 ET); ~9-10 full holidays a year plus 1:00 pm early closes (2026: Nov 27, Dec 24; Juneteenth Fri 2026-06-19 closed); a 9:30-16:00 session is 390 minutes = 78 left-labelled 5-minute bars (42 on half days), a 16:15-close option session 405 minutes = 81 bars (45); the 16:00 closing print spawns a spurious 79th bar unless folded into 15:55. → 06, 12

## 7. Strategies

- **0DTE strategies** — same-day credit spreads and iron condors sold at 5-15 delta, straddles after the opening range, lottery-ticket longs; theta is gone within hours and gamma is extreme, so P&L is dominated by a few large moves (a 0DTE condor with tight shorts and wide wings is erased by a 1% move) and on FOMC/CPI/NFP days IV crushes intraday; >95% of SPX 0DTE trades are capped-risk formats, ~4% naked short (Cboe, May 2025); aggregate retail 0DTE P&L is negative (~$350k/day, Beckmeyer-Branger-Gayda 2023 [secondary]); static 0DTE structures earn Sharpe ~0 after costs (Vilkov 2025 [uncertain]). → 07, 08, 11, 05, 13
- **Backspread / ratio (front) spread** — ratio 1x2 front spread: buy 1 near, sell 2 farther, same expiry, near zero cost, max profit = width + credit at the short strike, naked beyond it; backspread is the reverse (sell 1 near, buy 2 farther): +gamma +vega, loss zone at the long strike at expiry, open-ended beyond. → 07
- **Breakeven / POP / probability of touch** — breakeven = expiry price where P&L = 0 (long call K + debit; short put K - credit; credit spread short strike -/+ credit); POP = probability of profit, a risk-neutral approximation (~1 - credit/width for credit spreads; W = 5, C = 1.20 => ~76%), not a real-world probability and not expectancy; probability of touching a strike ~ 2x the probability of expiring ITM (reflection principle), so a 16-delta short strike is touched about one time in three and either side of a 16-delta strangle about half the time. → 07, 13
- **Butterfly / iron butterfly / broken-wing butterfly** (BFLY) — long butterfly: buy 1 lower, sell 2 middle, buy 1 upper, equal widths, max profit width - debit at the middle, a cheap pin bet; iron butterfly: short ATM straddle plus wings, max profit = credit, BE = K +/- credit; broken-wing: unequal wings for a small credit so one side carries no risk (risk beyond the far wing = (far - near width) - credit); Greeks: short gamma/vega near the body, long outside the wings, volga +59 at the body in the 30-day example. → 07, 03
- **Buying-power reduction** (BPR) — the capital a broker locks under its margin regime, the real cost of a credit trade; expands with vol and losses (50% usage in calm can exceed 100% after a -5% / +20-vol-point day with no new trades; 2-3x within days in Feb 2018 and March 2020); practitioner guidance keeps undefined-risk BPR at 25-50% of net liquidation (<= 30-35% in low-VIX regimes [folklore]). → 07, 13
- **Calendar / diagonal / double diagonal** — calendar (horizontal): same strike, short front / long back (long raw vega, short gamma, long theta; weighted-vega sign can flip); diagonal: different strikes and expiries; double diagonal: put diagonal below plus call diagonal above, condor-shaped with +vega; calendars exploit vol crush because longer expiries dilute the event lump; reverse calendars are margined as naked under Reg T; profit-taking 10-50% of debit. → 07, 03
- **Cash-secured put / naked short / covered call** — CSP: short put with K x 100 in cash or T-bills (benchmark PUT); naked/uncovered: short option with neither deliverable nor reserved cash, margined under Reg T or PM; covered call (buy-write): long 100 shares plus a short call entered as one order, net delta 0.5-0.8, max profit (K - S0) + credit, BE = S0 - credit; by parity a covered call is a short put: S - C(K) = K e^{-rT} + PV(D) - P(K). → 07, 01
- **Collar** (CLL1M / CLL3M) — stock plus long OTM put plus short OTM call, often zero-cost via skew, payoff bounded [K_put, K_call], delta ~0.3-0.6; Cboe CLL3M = 95/110 rolled quarterly, CLL1M = 95/100 monthly (read 2026-10-14; legacy 5% put / 10% call description not found); a very tight collar on appreciated stock risks constructive-sale treatment (IRC 1259; practitioners cite ~15-20% bands [uncertain]). → 07
- **Conversion / reversal / synthetic** — synthetic long stock = long call + short put (same K, T) less financing, reverse for short; conversion = long stock + long put + short call (riskless, earns the implied rate), reversal the mirror; early assignment on the American short leg breaks the fixed payoff. → 07, 02
- **Credit / debit position; defined / undefined risk; short / long premium** — credit = net premium received, debit = net premium paid (max loss = debit); defined risk = worst case known at entry (longs, verticals, iron structures), undefined = loss bounded only by zero (short put) or unbounded (short call); short premium = -gamma, -vega, +theta, long premium the reverse; defined risk is not small risk (a credit below 1/4 of width needs POP > 75% before costs just to break even). → 07, 13
- **Dispersion trade** — short index vol/correlation, long single-name vol in index (vega or correlation) weights; first-order P&L ~ N (rho_imp - rho_real)(sum w_i sigma_i)^2; pays because implied correlation (see Implied correlation) exceeds realized on average (the correlation risk premium, Driessen-Maenhout-Vilkov 2009) and loses when correlation goes to 1 in crashes; desks are long single-stock gamma and short index gamma (adds to index short gamma near popular strikes); alpha survives frictionlessly but not after realistic costs (DMV 2009). → 07, 04, 05, 08, 10, 11
- **Early-management rules** (45 DTE / 50% / 21 DTE / 25% straddles / 2x-credit stop) — retail short-premium convention: enter 30-60 DTE ("45 DTE") at ~16 delta, take profit at 50% of credit (25% for straddles; 10-50% calendars/iron flies), close or roll near 21 DTE, stop at ~2x credit received [tastylive pages not retrievable]; rationale is variance and tail reduction (gamma/theta worsens inside ~3 weeks), not better theta per gamma; 50% management raises win rate and P&L per day but lowers P&L per trade; whether 21 beats 14 or 30 DTE is not robust (in-sample choice); a 50%-profit / 2C-stop rule needs p >= 0.80 before costs. → 07, 03, 13
- **Earnings strategies / implied move** — jump variance v_j from two post-event expiries (decomposition: see Event variance), straddle-implied move ~0.8 sqrt(v_j) (80% at 7d and 45% at 35d => jump SD ~10.2%, straddle-implied ~8.2%; a normal move exceeds the straddle-implied move ~42% of the time); straddles entered 3 days before and exited on the announcement date earn positive returns (Gao-Xing-Zhang 2018, +3.34%), holding through is negative on average, and retail buyers pay 10%+ spreads and lose 5-14% (de Silva-Smith-So [secondary]); Nasdaq ISE skips Mon/Wed expiries on after-close earnings days. Full treatment belongs to the unwritten 14. → 07, 02, 04, 08, 11
- **Iron condor** (CNDR) — short strangle plus long wings; max loss = wider wing - credit; breakevens = short put - credit and short call + credit; Cboe CNDR sells ~20-delta put and call and buys ~5-delta wings monthly [uncertain]; condors earn less per unit of risk than strangles under PM but more per unit of BPR under Reg T; the put wing is bought at higher IV than the short put (skew cost). → 07
- **Jade lizard / reverse jade lizard** — short OTM put plus short OTM call vertical with total credit >= call-spread width (no upside risk, downside BE = K_put - credit); reverse: short call plus short put spread with credit >= put width (no downside risk, naked upside). → 07
- **Long call / long put / vertical** — long call: max loss = debit, BE = K + debit; long put: BE = K - debit, max profit K - debit; vertical = same expiry, different strikes (leg = one option or stock line): debit spread max loss = debit, max profit = width - debit; credit spread max profit = credit, max loss = width - credit; a 95/105 call vertical's gamma flips sign at the midpoint (K1 + K2)/2; early assignment on a vertical/diagonal turns the spread into stock plus a long option, possibly creating a Reg T call or weekend short-stock borrow and dividend liability. → 07, 03
- **Poor man's covered call / ZEBRA / the wheel** — PMCC: LEAPS diagonal, long deep-ITM call >= 1 year (70-85 delta) against short-dated OTM calls (LEAPS extrinsic collapses in a crash); ZEBRA (zero extrinsic back ratio): buy 2 ITM ~70-delta calls, sell 1 ATM call, same expiry, ~+100 delta with near-zero net extrinsic at inception only; the wheel: sell CSPs until assigned, then covered calls until called away, a path-dependent cycle that holds losers and surrenders winners. → 07
- **Protective put / tail hedging** (PPUT; Universa vs AQR) — protective put: long stock plus long put, floor K - debit; rolling a 5% OTM 1-month put costs several percent of NAV a year (2-5% [uncertain]; far-OTM programs ~0.5-2%) and cuts drawdowns less reliably than holding less equity at matched volatility (Israelov 2019, "Pathetic Protection"; PPUT 2022 -19.8% vs SPX -19.4%); tail hedging (Universa/Spitznagel): a small allocation to far-OTM SPX puts rolled systematically, a steady bleed for explosive payoffs (reported 3,612% on invested capital in March 2020 per investor letter, Forbes headline 4,144% [press-reported]; CalPERS dropped its program in early 2020); AQR's counter: because of the VRP systematic puts are negative-alpha and protection is path-dependent; 10 and 13 add that PPUT beats SPX only when spikes last weeks and gives gains back without an exit rule. → 07, 13, 10, 08
- **Risk reversal** (RXM) — long OTM call + short OTM put (bullish) or the reverse, near zero-cost via skew, stock-like outside the strikes; the cleanest listed skew expression when delta-hedged (large delta, small net vega, large vanna: ~+1.5 for a 30-day 90/110 reversal); 25-delta risk reversal in vol points = IV(25d put) - IV(25d call) in equity convention (FX quotes call minus put); SPX 1-month ~4-8 points normal, 10+ in stress [practitioner]. → 07, 03, 04
- **Rolling** — closing a position and reopening at another strike, expiry or both; only the net credit/debit of the two trades matters; rolling a deep-ITM short "for a credit" realizes the loss and re-bets it with more duration and size (roll chains are the path to catastrophic loss); "roll when tested" rules fire on most trades because touches are ~2x expiries. → 07, 13
- **Straddle / strangle** — long straddle: long call and put at one strike, BEs = K +/- debit, costs ~0.8 SD (the expected absolute move); short strangle convention 16-delta ("1-SD") at 45 DTE, wins ~68% under a lognormal with zero expected P&L before costs (positive expectancy comes only from IV exceeding realized); an ATM-spot straddle has small positive delta (0.068 in the example) because d1 > 0, so the zero-delta straddle is struck near the forward; Reg T margins a short strangle as the larger naked side plus the other short's value. → 07, 03, 13
- **VIX hedges** (VIX calls, VIX call spreads, VXTH) — hedge value ~ beta(future to spot) x spot move - roll, with front-month beta ~0.5-0.7 in contango [uncertain]; VIX calls beat SPX puts in fast crashes where futures follow spot (Feb 2018, March 2020) and are nearly worthless in slow grinds (2022: SPX ~-25%, VIX peak close 36.45); on 2024-08-05 spot printed ~65 while front futures peaked in the 30s [uncertain]; size by vega notional; in backwardation (VIX > ~30) prefer SPX put spreads or VIX call spreads over outright front-month calls. → 07, 04, 10, 13

- **T+1** — next-day settlement in the US, Canada and Mexico since 2024-05-28 (options were already T+1; the stock leg of exercise moved T+2 -> T+1); ex-date now equals record date, so dividend capture requires exercising by the broker cut-off the day before ex-date; EU/UK/Swiss T+1 scheduled 2027-10-11 [training]. → 09, 11, 01, 07
- **Tax rules for equity options** (wash sale, straddle, qualified covered call, constructive sale, good-faith violation) — wash sale (IRC 1091): buying a call or substantially identical option within 30 days before/after a loss sale, IRA replacement losses permanently disallowed (Rev. Rul. 2008-5); straddle (IRC 1092): offsetting positions defer losses and reset holding periods; qualified covered call (1092(c)(4)): > 30 days to expiry when written, term <= 33 months, strike not below the lowest qualified benchmark (<= $25: >= 85% of price; <= $150: >= price - $10; > 90-day calls above $150: second strike below the prior close); constructive sale (IRC 1259): a very tight collar may be treated as a sale; capital-loss deduction $3,000/yr (1211(b)); good-faith violation: selling in a cash account with unsettled proceeds, three in 12 months restricts the account. → 07

## 9. Data and methodology

- **Anomaly decay / multiple-testing hurdle** (McLean-Pontiff 2016; Harvey-Liu-Zhu 2016; Hou-Xue-Zhang 2020) — published cross-sectional predictors fall from 0.58%/month in-sample to 0.40% out-of-sample (-26%, data mining) and 0.26% post-publication (-58%, arbitrage), worst 3-4 years after publication, so a live signal is worth ~0.4-0.5x in-sample before costs; with 316 factors from 313 papers a t of 2.0 no longer controls false discoveries, so require |t| > 3.0 (single-test p < ~0.0027); 65% of 452 anomalies fail |t| >= 1.96 once microcaps are controlled. → 08, 12
- **Binomial intervals / sample size** (Clopper-Pearson; Wilson; design effect) — Clopper-Pearson 95% interval from Beta quantiles (exact, conservative; 60 of 100 => [0.497, 0.697]; 220 of 400 => [0.500, 0.600]); Wilson interval has better average coverage (Brown, Cai & DasGupta 2001); hit-rate SE = sqrt(p(1 - p)/n); one-proportion sample size n = (z_a sqrt(p0(1 - p0)) + z_b sqrt(p1(1 - p1)))^2/(p1 - p0)^2 (0.50 vs 0.55 => 783; vs 0.52 => 4,903); design effect n_eff = n/(1 + (m - 1) rho) for clustered touches; a 5-point binary hit-rate edge needs ~800 independent sessions at 80% power. → 12
- **Block bootstrap / HAC / Newey-West** — standard errors and resampling that respect serial dependence: Newey-West weights w_j = 1 - j/(L + 1) with L = floor(4 (T/100)^{2/9}) or floor(0.75 T^{1/3}); block length ~T^{1/3} or Politis-White automatic; stationary bootstrap with geometric blocks of mean 1/p; overlapping 21-day windows need HAC lag >= 21 or a block bootstrap (n days ~ n/21 independent observations). → 12, 04
- **Brier score / proper scoring rules** (Murphy decomposition; BSS; log loss; ECE; RPS) — proper scoring rule = expected score optimized only by the true probability (Brier and log loss strictly proper, hit rate not); BS = (1/N) sum (f_i - o_i)^2 = reliability - resolution + uncertainty (Murphy 1973); Brier skill score BSS = 1 - BS/obar(1 - obar) (a constant 0.60 forecast at a 0.55 base rate gives BSS = -0.0101); log loss with probabilities clipped to [1e-6, 1 - 1e-6]; expected calibration error = bin-weighted mean |obar_k - f_k| (report 10 equal-width or quantile bins); ranked probability score for ordered categories; hit rate can be maximized by predicting the majority class while BSS is negative. → 12
- **Climatology / placebo levels / event study** — climatology = the unconditional outcome frequency in the same context (session-time bucket, vol regime), the base rate every level or signal claim is scored against (skill = 1 - score/score_climatology); placebo levels = the identical pipeline run on fake reference prices or matched non-event windows; event study = abnormal return (realized minus expected) around an event with CARs, clustered standard errors by session because intraday touches cluster; the only legitimate route to any key-level claim. → 12, 06, 08
- **COT / TFF; short interest vs short volume** — CFTC Commitments of Traders (Tuesday positions, Friday 3:30 pm ET release) and the Traders in Financial Futures report for VIX futures (code 1170E1, from 2006-06-13: Dealer/Intermediary, Asset Manager, Leveraged Funds, Other Reportables); FINRA short interest = positions reported twice monthly (Rule 4560), daily short-sale volume = the flow of off-exchange shorts including market-maker hedging (consolidated files from 2018-08-01 [uncertain]). → 12, 10
- **Day-count and time-to-expiry conventions** — T = (calendar days + intraday fraction)/365 or (trading days + fraction)/252, with sigma_365 sqrt(T_365) = sigma_252 sqrt(T_252) at a fixed price (Friday-to-Monday: sigma_252/sigma_365 = 1.44); intraday fraction = minutes to the contract's expiry reference / 390 (210 or 225 on early-close days); AM-settled series effectively expire at the Friday open, so Thursday-afternoon T includes Curb and the overnight; mixing conventions at the front end moves 0-3 DTE IVs by several vol points. → 12, 02
- **Deflated Sharpe Ratio** (DSR; PSR; MinTRL; Bailey & Lopez de Prado 2014) — probabilistic Sharpe ratio PSR(SR*) = Phi((SR - SR*) sqrt(T - 1)/sqrt(1 - g3 SR + ((g4 - 1)/4) SR^2)) using skew g3 and kurtosis g4; DSR = PSR evaluated against the expected maximum Sharpe of N trials SR* = sqrt(V)[(1 - gamma) Phi^{-1}(1 - 1/N) + gamma Phi^{-1}(1 - 1/(N e))], gamma = 0.5772 (the max of N zero-skill Sharpes grows like sqrt(2 ln N)); worked example SR 1.0/yr, 3 years daily, N = 100: PSR(0) = 0.956 but DSR = 0.33; minimum track record length 2.8 years for SR 1.0, 11.0 years for SR 0.5 (same moments, 95%); walk-forward prevents lookahead but not selection, so DSR/PBO must count every variant tried. → 12
- **Diebold-Mariano test** (HLN correction; Giacomini-White) — tests whether the mean loss differential between two forecasts is zero: DM = mean(d)/sqrt(HAC var(d)/N) ~ N(0, 1), with the Harvey-Leybourne-Newbold small-sample factor sqrt((N + 1 - 2h + h(h - 1)/N)/N); two Brier point scores without a DM test are not a comparison. → 12
- **Fill realism / transaction-cost model** — cost per option leg = phi x half-spread + commission + fees with phi in [0.25, 1.0] (mid +/- 25-50% of the half-spread for liquid series, at the touch when the spread > 10% of mid; desk rule ~30-40% of the spread from mid); itemize per-contract fees (~$0.03-0.10 [uncertain]); simulate assignment and expiry handling for SPY; backtests at mid prices are not realistic. → 12, 07, 08
- **IV convention set / quote filters / trade signing** — vendor IVs are comparable only if forward, rate, day-count, intraday clock, model (European BSM vs American binomial), input (mid vs last) and tick match; before computing IV drop bid = 0 series (far-OTM bids are permanently zero), spreads > max(2 ticks, 20% of mid), quotes older than 60 s and the 9:30-9:35 window, and carry a bid-IV/ask-IV band for premiums under ~5 ticks; before flow statistics exclude or tag multi-leg legs, stock-option legs, auction/price-improvement prints and cancels (double-digit percent of SPX prints [uncertain]) and sign trades by the quote rule at the NBBO mid, since the Lee-Ready tick rule is unreliable for options (Muravyev 2016). → 12, 02, 06
- **Lookahead bias / survivorship bias / structural breaks** — lookahead = using information unavailable at decision time (bar close inside the bar, higher-timeframe values before close, future-adjusted prices, realized SPY dividends before declaration, same-session OI; Pine `request.security` with `lookahead_on` repaints); survivorship = a universe built from today's constituents; structural-break calendar for US options: SPX Wednesday/Monday weeklies 2016, Penny Program permanent 2020-07-01, GTH ~24x5 2021-11-21, SPX Tue/Thu 2022-04/05, SPY/QQQ/IWM Tue/Thu 2022-11, round-lot redefinition 2025-11-03 [several uncertain]; never fit a 0DTE model on pre-2022-05 data without a regime flag. → 12, 06
- **Open interest timing / listing-day artefact** — OI attached to session D is positions as of the D-1 close, delivered before the open (publication time uncertain); a series listed on day D shows OI = 0; same-day positioning must come from volume plus open/close flags; 0DTE OI is created and extinguished within the session. → 12, 01, 05
- **OPRA data schemas / sale conditions / vendors** — OPRA disseminates per-exchange BBOs with the SIP-computed NBBO as appendages, only on change (illiquid BBOs can be hours old), with per-trade sale conditions (regular, cancel/correction, late, single leg of a multi-leg order, stock-option leg, auction/price-improvement, floor); Databento OPRA.PILLAR cbbo-1m/trades/OHLCV history from 2013-04-01, tick-level cmbp-1/tcbbo/cbbo-1s from 2023-03-28 (tens of GB/day per liquid underlying [uncertain]); Cboe DataShop Option Intervals from Jan 2012; OptionMetrics IvyDB US EOD from Jan 1996 (kernel-smoothed surface at deltas 10-90, tenors 10 days-2 years; Intraday from 2018 at 10:00/14:00/15:45 ET); ORATS from 2007 [uncertain]; ThetaData tiers back to 2012-06-01 (CTA-tape stocks such as SPY only from 2020-01-01); Massive (ex-Polygon, renamed 2025-10-30); IB's historical API has no expired-option history and strict pacing limits; TradingView's non-upgraded real-time feed is Cboe One, not the SIP [uncertain]. → 12
- **Pre-registration / frozen protocol / sealed window** — hypotheses, sample, outcomes, metrics, decision rules and code fixed, hashed and timestamped before sealed data are touched, with one holdout opening; this repository's NME-001 record seals 2022-06-22..2024-12-31; pin dependencies, hash and snapshot raw data because deterministic code alone is not reproducible. → 12, 08
- **Purged K-fold / embargo / CPCV / PBO** (Lopez de Prado 2018) — purging drops training samples whose label windows overlap the test window; embargo drops training samples just after it (h = int(pctEmbargo x T), default 1% of the full sample, AFML Snippet 7.2); combinatorial purged cross-validation tests every combination of k of N groups (N = 6, k = 2 => 15 splits, 5 OOS paths) to get a distribution of OOS performance; probability of backtest overfitting = share of CSCV splits where the in-sample-best configuration is below median OOS; walk-forward (Pardo 2008) refits on rolling/anchored windows and stitches OOS blocks (WFE = annualised OOS/IS return) but yields one path. → 12
- **Timestamps and joins** (ts_event vs ts_recv; as-of joins; UTC) — exchange/SIP event time vs vendor receipt time differ by microseconds to milliseconds; pair option quotes with the underlying by backward as-of joins on a common UTC clock with tolerance (~60 s), never a 4:00 stock close with a 4:15 option quote; during GTH pair with the ES mid at the same instant (S_implied = F_ES e^{-(r - q) T_ES}); store in UTC, Parquet with ~1e6-row groups, dictionary-encoded OSI symbols, partitioned by underlying and session date. → 12
- **Variance-time clock** — time to expiry measured in expected variance rather than minutes: T_var(t) = sum of remaining intraday bucket weights (from a >= 60-session 5-minute RV profile, one full day = 1/252) + w_overnight x overnights + w_event x scheduled events; the first and last 30 minutes each carry ~2-3x a midday bucket and the overnight ~20-40% [uncertain]; the practitioner default for DTE <= 5 because wall-clock T makes 0DTE IV drift mechanically through the day. → 12, 03, 04

## 10. Risk and behaviour

- **Behavioural biases** (prospect theory, loss aversion, disposition effect, overconfidence, lottery preference, anchoring, narrow framing, break-even effect, house-money effect, outcome bias) — prospect theory (Tversky-Kahneman 1992): value v(x) = x^0.88 for gains, -2.25(-x)^0.88 for losses (losses weigh ~2x), probability weighting w(p) = p^g/(p^g + (1 - p)^g)^{1/g} with g = 0.61 gains / 0.69 losses, so small probabilities are overweighted (far-OTM options and MAX stocks overpriced, Bali-Cakici-Whitelaw 2011 ~-1%/month); disposition effect = sell winners, hold losers (Odean 1998 PGR 0.148 vs PLR 0.098); overconfidence raises turnover and transfers wealth to the spread (Barber-Odean 2000: most active quintile 11.4% vs 17.9% market); narrow framing sizes each trade alone so ten 2% trades become a 20% correlated book; break-even effect (Thaler-Johnson 1990) underlies revenge trading and rolling losers; house-money effect raises risk after gains; outcome bias ("resulting") judges a decision by its outcome; remedies are pre-commitment rules, journals and kill switches. → 13
- **Conditional liquidity / correlation-to-1** — market makers scale quoted depth to RV and inventory, so quotes widen 5-10x or vanish when RV jumps; in liquidity shocks a common deleveraging factor drives pairwise correlations toward 0.8-0.9+ (2008, March 2020, 2024 rotation [uncertain]), index vol approaches single-stock vol, dispersion books and diversified short-put books collapse into one leveraged index exposure. → 10, 13, 05
- **Expectancy / breakeven win rate** — E = p x W - (1 - p) x L; breakeven W/L = (1 - p)/p (p = 0.70 => 0.43; 0.90 => 0.11); a 90% POP trade losing 15x its credit is negative expectancy; "selling premium wins 70-80% so it is positive expectancy" confuses win rate with the complement of the tail area. → 13, 07
- **Fat tails / excess kurtosis / jump and gap risk** — kurtosis = standardized fourth moment (normal 3, excess 0); SPX daily log-return kurtosis 19.8 (2016-2026), excess kurtosis since 1950 on the order of 20-30 with 1987 [uncertain]; |r| > 4% days number 50-100 since 1950 vs ~1 expected under a normal; normal two-sided tail frequencies: 2 sigma 1 day in 22, 3 sigma 1 in 370, 4 sigma 1 in 15,787 (~63 years), 6 sigma 1 in 507 million; jump risk = a discontinuous move that cannot be hedged through, gap risk its overnight/weekend form (the margin call is against a price that cannot be improved; stops become market orders at the open). → 13, 04, 03
- **Greek limits / gamma window / normalized vega** — book governed by dollar gamma per 1% move, dollar vega per vol point by expiry bucket (0-7d / 8-30d / 31-90d / 90d+), normalized vega (Vega x sqrt(30/DTE)), beta-weighted delta and the theta/gamma breakeven; conservative retail limits $Gamma(1%) and $Vega each <= 1-2% of NLV [uncertain]; set gamma limits in sigma-scaled moves (a 1% move is ~1.6 sigma at 10 vol, ~0.5 sigma at 30 vol: a fixed-% limit allows 9x more realized-move P&L at 30 vol); the gamma window = the last 2-3 weeks before expiry where ATM gamma ~1/(S sigma sqrt(T)) concentrates risk. → 13, 03
- **Kelly criterion / fractional Kelly / risk of ruin** — discrete Kelly f* = p - q/b (p = 0.70, win $100, loss $300 => f* = -0.20: do not bet; loss $150 => 0.25, quarter-Kelly ~6%); continuous f* = mu/sigma^2 with growth g(c) = r + (mu^2/sigma^2)(c - c^2/2) (mu 5%, sigma 15% => 2.22x leverage; half-Kelly keeps 75% of growth at half the vol; 2x Kelly gives zero growth); P(equity ever reaches fraction x) = x^{2/c - 1} (halving: 50% at full Kelly, 12.5% at half, 0.8% at quarter); even-money ruin (q/p)^N; use at most half-Kelly and quarter-Kelly when the edge rests on fewer than ~5 years of data; negatively skewed payoffs understate true Kelly risk. → 13
- **Liquidity sizing / notional exposure** — size per strike/expiry <= 1-5% of open interest and a few percent of ADV, days-to-liquidate = n/(ADV share per day), NLV haircut = 0.5 x (3x normal spread) x 100 x n per short leg, mark at the far side [uncertain]; a short put is long stock worth K x 100 per contract on the downside (10 short SPY 500 puts = $500,000 notional, 5x on $100,000; a 20% gap loses ~$100,000 less premium); theta traders routinely carry 3-6x hidden long-equity leverage. → 13
- **Margin expansion / margin spiral / liquidation mechanics** — BPR (see Buying-power reduction) grows with vol and losses because TIMS revalues at live IV and brokers add house scenarios intraday (March 2020, Nov 2020 election); margins rise with vol, forcing deleveraging that raises vol (Brunnermeier-Pedersen 2009; max position = Capital/m(sigma), doubling sigma halves the position): VIX-futures margin hikes Feb 2018, NSCC's $6.9bn intraday calls on 2021-01-27 and Clearing Fund jump $26bn -> $33.5bn on 01-28 (Robinhood restrictions), LDI collateral calls 2022; liquidation routines close the largest margin consumers first by buying at the ask in thin markets, and an early-assigned spread leg can create a Reg T call that exceeds net liq; size to the stressed requirement, not the entry requirement. → 13, 10, 07, 09
- **Operational / counterparty risk** — wrong strike/expiry/right/quantity/side, AM vs PM settlement confusion, legging risk, outages (Robinhood 2020-03-02/03; Knight Capital $440m in 45 minutes, 2012-08-01), forced-liquidation mechanics, box spreads on American underlyings; counterparty risk of a listed option is to OCC via novation, custody risk is covered by SIPC, and no intermediary (broker, market maker, exchange, finfluencer) bears the customer's tail. → 13, 09
- **Short-vol supply chain / picking up pennies** — suppliers of index vol: autocallables, covered-call and buffer ETFs, pension/insurer overwriting, hedge-fund carry shorts, inverse VIX ETPs, 0DTE sellers, market makers when customers net buy; demanders: tail-hedge funds, pension overlays, vol-target hedges, retail call buyers, long-VIX ETPs, dealers hedging structured books; the carry is paid for convex, path-dependent losses concentrated on the worst-liquidity days ("picking up pennies in front of a steamroller": high win rate, small gains, rare catastrophic loss), and many sellers are also forced de-leveragers, so supply disappears when demand spikes; short vol stops being compensated when size makes a 1-in-10-year event fatal, crowding compresses the premium, or costs consume it; a short-vol Sharpe above ~1.5 over fewer than ten years is presumed to contain unpriced tail risk; survivorship bias: failed funds leave the databases. → 10, 13, 05
- **Sizing rules** (vol-based, fixed-fraction, fixed-dollar, volatility targeting, drawdown asymmetry, per-trade limits) — vol-based size = risk budget/(notional x forecast sigma) understates option risk because trailing RV is lowest before shocks and short-gamma P&L is quadratic (a 1% move costing $1,000 via gamma costs ~$25,000 at 5%); fixed-fraction risks a constant % of current equity (contracts = floor(equity x r/max loss per contract)), fixed-dollar does not compound or shrink; vol targeting scales exposure to sigma_target/sigma_forecast with a cap; a drawdown d needs a gain of d/(1 - d) to recover (50% => 100%, 7.3 years at 10% CAGR); per-trade 0.5-2% defined-risk, 1-5% undefined-risk on the stress cell, 5-10% per underlying, 10-15% per correlated theme; kill switch at 2-3% daily loss with a drawdown ladder (-10% halve size, -20% flat). → 13
- **Stress matrix / VaR / expected shortfall** — full revaluation over a joint grid of spot {-20, -10, -5, 0, +5, +10, +20}% x IV {-10, -5, 0, +5, +10, +20} points with overlays (front IV shocked 2x back, 25-delta put skew +3 to +5 points, correlation 1 via beta, spreads 3x, margin per cell), reporting the worst-cell P&L and margin; worst case for undefined risk = (K - premium) x 100 x n for puts, +50-100% single name / +20% index for calls; historical replay set (1987-10-19/20, Oct 2008, 2011-08-08, 2015-08-24, 2018-02-05/06, 2020-02-24..03-16, 2022-11-10, 2024-08-05, 2025-04-03/04/09); VaR = 1-day loss quantile that says nothing beyond it, expected shortfall = mean loss beyond VaR (replaced VaR under Basel FRTB, 2019); delta-normal VaR is wrong on both linearity and constant vol for short gamma/vega. → 13, 03
